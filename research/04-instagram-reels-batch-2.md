**Techniques**
- **Source verification on camera**: "maine dost se poocha, khud shoot ki?" Isse authenticity
  aati hai, aur yeh fake-video waale R1 se juda hua trust signal hai.
- **"Kadwa sach"** ek repeat phrase hai jo controversial point se pehle aata hai.

- **Surprise data point** ("Barnala?!") aur sabhi governments ko ek saath CTA, taaki "sab party"
  par barabar pressure lage.

- "Do hi main cause, dono 15–20%" ka matlab hai ki baaki 60–70% (dust, industry, construction)

  wala conclusion kamzor hai.
- Vehicles ko "nahi rok sakte" kehna aur parali ko "rok sakte ho" kehna ek policy choice hai,
  fact nahi. Public transport aur emission norms jaise vehicle-side options skip ho gaye.
- "Punjab se zyada videos" anecdotal evidence hai. Fire-count data (satellite: NASA FIRMS,
  CREAMS / IARI) behtar saboot hota.
- Barnala ka AQI isliye high hai kyunki aag wahin paas lagti hai. Iska population se lena-dena
  nahi hai.
