## 4. Openly stated beliefs and positioning (podcast se, unke apne shabdon ka saar)

  chhupate nahi.

  sab galat ho sakte hain. Jo us area mein reh chuka hai, wahi asli dard samajhta hai.
- **Research khud karte hain.** "Public mera chehra dekh ke trust karti hai, toh research

- **TV news ke against.** "Ek hi news ko aadha ghanta kheenchte hain, kehne ko kuch hai hi nahi."
- **Quality > frequency.** Nitish Rajput ki tareef: "mahine mein ek video, par loophole nahi
  chhodta, documentary jaisi."

## 5. Strengths (yeh seekhna hai)

- **Curiosity-first hooks.** Ek naam ya term se shuru karte hain jo viewer ne suna toh hai,
  par samajhta nahi.
- **Relevance bridge.** Har badi story ko viewer ke phone ya life se jod dete hain.
- **Proof on screen.** News headlines, photos aur logos turant dikhate hain.
- **Personal research journey.** "Maine padha, sab ek hi story bata rahe the, par mujhe
  kuch aur mila." Isse viewer ko lagta hai woh surface se neeche ja rahe hain.
- **Conversational Hindi.** Script padhte hue nahi lagte, dost ko samjhaate hue lagte hain.
- **Strong opinion.** Neutral reporter nahi hain, unka ek clear stand hota hai.

## 6. Weaknesses and criticism (yahan aap unse aage nikal sakte ho)

- Critics ne "biased" aur "facts kitne sach hain?" jaise sawaal uthaaye hain (jaise
  "Rishabh Bidhuri Exposed" type videos). Matlab **fact accuracy unka weak flank hai.**
- "Ground reality" wala argument emotional roop se strong hai, par poori community ke baare
  mein generalisation ki taraf ja sakta hai. Isse platform strikes aur trust loss ka risk
  badhta hai.
- Rival creators se personal fights reach toh laati hain, par brand ko "controversy channel"
  bana sakti hain.

**Aapka edge:** inki hook aur storytelling technique lo, aur usmein do cheezein jodo:
**(a) har claim ka source screen par**, aur **(b) doosre paksh ka strongest argument
(steelman)**. Isse aap "thoughtful + impactful" dono banoge, sirf "impactful" nahi.
