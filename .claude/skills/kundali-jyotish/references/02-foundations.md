Natural benefics: Jupiter, Venus, waxing Moon, unafflicted Mercury. Natural malefics: Saturn, Mars,
Rahu, Ketu, Sun (mild), waning Moon, afflicted Mercury.

Gender and speed matter for timing: Moon ~13°/day (2.25 days/sign), Sun ~1°/day (1 month/sign),
Mercury and Venus ~1 month/sign (variable), Mars ~1.5-2 months/sign (6+ when retrograde), Jupiter
~1 year/sign, Saturn ~2.5 years/sign, Rahu/Ketu ~1.5 years/sign (always retrograde).

## 5. Dignity and strength

Order of dignity: deep exaltation > exalted > moolatrikona > own > great friend > friend > neutral
> enemy > great enemy > debilitated. (Exaltation: Sun Aries 10°, Moon Taurus 3°, Mars Capricorn 28°,
Mercury Virgo 15°, Jupiter Cancer 5°, Venus Pisces 27°, Saturn Libra 20°; debilitation is the
opposite point.)

How to judge overall strength (no single score decides; triangulate):
1. Sign dignity in D1 and D9 (D9 shows the "fruit"; a debilitated D1 planet that is strong in D9
   recovers; a vargottama planet is reliable).
2. House: kendra/trikona strong, dusthana weak (except malefics in 3/6/11, which work well).
3. Dig bala: Jupiter/Mercury in 1st, Sun/Mars in 10th, Saturn in 7th, Moon/Venus in 4th.
4. Combustion (weakens significations, especially of Mercury/Venus for relationships and
   communication), planetary war (loser weakened), retrogression (strong but erratic; results
   arrive with revisions, delays or a "second time").
5. Aspects from benefics/malefics, and the strength of the dispositor (sign lord).
6. Shadbala from the report if present: ratio ≥1 = adequate; ≥1.5 = very strong. Bhava bala ranks
   houses. Vimshopaka (out of 20) is another summary.
7. Avastha (Baladi): Yuva (adult) gives full results; Bala/Vriddha partial; Mrita little.
8. SAV/BAV: a planet in a sign where its own BAV is ≥5 performs well.

Moon's strength also depends on paksha: bright (near Purnima) is strong, dark (near Amavasya) weak.

## 6. Functional benefics and malefics by Lagna (Parashari summary)

Principles: lords of trikonas (1,5,9) are benefic; a planet owning a kendra and a trikona is a
**yogakaraka**; lords of 3,6,11 are malefic; the 8th lord is malefic unless it is also the Lagna
lord; natural benefics owning kendras lose beneficence (kendradhipati dosha); lords of 2 and 7
are marakas (health-care significators, not death predictors); the 12th lord gives results
according to its other house and placement.

| Aries | - | Sun, Jupiter, Mars (L) | Mercury, Saturn | Venus |
| Taurus | Saturn | Saturn, Sun, Mercury, Venus (L) | Jupiter, Moon, Mars | Mars, Mercury |
| Gemini | - | Venus, Mercury (L), Saturn (mixed) | Mars, Sun, Jupiter | Moon, Jupiter |
| Cancer | Mars | Mars, Moon (L), Jupiter | Venus, Mercury, Saturn | Saturn, Sun |
| Leo | Mars | Mars, Sun (L), Jupiter | Mercury, Venus, Saturn | Saturn, Mercury |
| Virgo | - | Venus, Mercury (L) | Mars, Moon, Jupiter | Jupiter, Venus |
| Libra | Saturn | Saturn, Mercury, Venus (L) | Jupiter, Sun, Mars | Mars |

is a starting point: a functional malefic that is strong and well placed can still give good
worldly results in its house; always read placement and strength.

## 7. Aspects and conjunctions

- Graha drishti (whole-sign): all planets aspect the 7th sign from them. Mars also 4th and 8th,
  Jupiter 5th and 9th, Saturn 3rd and 10th. Rahu/Ketu: 5th/7th/9th in the popular view.
- Jaimini rashi drishti (sign aspects): movable signs aspect fixed signs except the adjacent one;
  fixed aspect movable except the adjacent one; dual signs aspect each other.
- Conjunctions blend natures; the planet with higher dignity/strength dominates. Closer (within
  ~5°) = stronger effect. Common blends: Sun-Mercury (intellect, common), Moon-Jupiter (Gajakesari
  flavour, kindness), Mars-Saturn (friction, hard work), Venus-Saturn (late but stable love), Moon-
  Rahu (restless mind), Jupiter-Rahu (unorthodox beliefs, guru issues), Mars-Rahu (impulsive
  energy, accidents - drive carefully).

## 8. Special states

- **Retrograde**: closer to Earth, brighter, "strong but turns back" - results come after
  re-attempts or with a reversal. Retrograde in exaltation sign behaves partly like debilitation in
  some views; do not overstate.
- **Combust**: Mercury is combust in most charts (it never strays far from the Sun); judge it
  lightly. Combust Venus affects relationship comforts more noticeably.
- **Planetary war (graha yuddha)**: two of Mars, Mercury, Jupiter, Venus, Saturn within 1°. The
  loser (traditionally the one with lower ecliptic latitude / smaller disc) is weakened in its dasha.
- **Vargottama**: same sign in D1 and D9 - stable, reliable, delivers its promise.
- **Pushkara navamsa/bhaga**: auspicious degrees (optional detail; only mention if the report shows it).
