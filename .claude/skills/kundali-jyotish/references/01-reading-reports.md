## 1. What a typical report contains

| Section (English / Hindi) | What to extract | Watch out for |
|---|---|---|
| Basic details / जन्म विवरण | name, sex, DOB, TOB, place, lat/long, time zone, ayanamsa, sunrise | 12h vs 24h time; DD/MM vs MM/DD; "Ayanamsa: KP" changes every cusp |
| Avakahada chakra / अवकहड़ा चक्र | rashi, nakshatra-charan, varna, vashya, yoni, gana, nadi, tithi, yoga, karana, name letter | Varna is a matching label from the Moon sign, not caste |
| Planetary positions / ग्रह स्पष्ट | sign, degree, nakshatra, pada, lord, house, R/C markers | degrees may be absolute (0-360) or within the sign; "Rashi" column may be a number 1-12 |
| Lagna kundali / लग्न कुंडली (D1) | houses and occupants | do not confuse with Chandra kundali or Bhava chalit |
| Chandra kundali / चंद्र कुंडली | same chart with Moon's sign as house 1 | useful cross-check, not the main chart |
| Navamsa / नवांश (D9), other vargas (D2-D60) | sign of each planet in each varga | some reports list vargas as a table only |
| Bhava chalit / भाव चलित | planets shifted by house cusps | a planet can move one house; Parashari reading still uses D1 by sign |
| Vimshottari dasha / विंशोत्तरी दशा | MD/AD/PD tables with dates | date format; year length (365.25 vs 360) |
| Yogini / Char dasha | alternative dasha tables | note them; Vimshottari stays primary |
| Ashtakavarga / अष्टकवर्ग | BAV per planet, SAV per sign | SAV may be listed by sign (Aries first) or by house (Lagna first) |
| Shadbala / षड्बल | strength in rupas, ratio to required | ratio >1 means strong enough |
| KP tables | cusps, star lord, sub lord, significators | KP ayanamsa and Placidus cusps |
| Manglik / Kaal Sarp / Sade Sati / gemstone pages | software's conclusions | treat as claims to verify, not facts |
| Lal Kitab pages | remedies and "teva" | a separate system; do not mix with Parashari logic |

## 2. Chart layouts

**North Indian (diamond) - houses fixed, signs move.** The top-centre diamond is always house 1.
Houses run **anticlockwise**: 2 = top-left triangle, 3 = left-upper triangle, 4 = left diamond,
5 = left-lower triangle, 6 = bottom-left triangle, 7 = bottom diamond, 8 = bottom-right triangle,
9 = right-lower triangle, 10 = right diamond, 11 = right-upper triangle, 12 = top-right triangle.
The small **numbers are sign numbers** (1 Aries ... 12 Pisces), not house numbers. The number in the
top diamond is the Lagna sign.

| Position in the square | House |
|---|---|
| top centre diamond | 1 |
| top edge, LEFT of centre (triangle) | 2 |
| left edge, upper triangle | 3 |
| left centre diamond | 4 |
| left edge, lower triangle | 5 |
| bottom edge, left of centre | 6 |
| bottom centre diamond | 7 |
| bottom edge, right of centre | 8 |
| right edge, lower triangle | 9 |
| right centre diamond | 10 |
| right edge, upper triangle | 11 |
| top edge, RIGHT of centre | 12 |

Check: house 4 (left diamond) and house 10 (right diamond) face each other, as do 1 and 7.

**South Indian (grid) - signs fixed, houses move.** 4x4 grid with the centre empty. Signs run
**clockwise** starting with Pisces at top-left, Aries next to it: top row Pis, Ari, Tau, Gem; right
column Can, Leo; bottom row (right to left) Vir, Lib, Sco, Sag; left column (bottom to top) Cap, Aqu.
The Lagna box is marked "La", "Asc", "ल" or with a diagonal line. Count houses clockwise from it.

**East Indian (Bengal/Odisha)** - signs fixed in a different layout with Aries at the top centre.
If you meet one, do not guess the direction: rebuild the chart from the planet table.

Golden rule: **the planetary table wins.** Rebuild the chart from table degrees and check it
matches the drawing; drawings are where misreadings happen.

## 3. Abbreviations

| Planet | English | Hindi / Devanagari | Also |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sun | Su, Sy | सू, सूर्य, र (Ravi) | Surya, Ravi |
| Moon | Mo, Ch | चं, चन्द्र | Chandra, Soma |
| Mars | Ma, Ku | मं, मंगल, भौ | Mangal, Kuja, Bhauma |
| Mercury | Me, Bu | बु, बुध | Budh |
| Jupiter | Ju, Gu, Br | गु, बृ, गुरु | Guru, Brihaspati |
| Venus | Ve, Sk | शु, शुक्र | Shukra |
| Saturn | Sa | श, शनि | Shani |
| Rahu | Ra | रा | Dragon's head |
| Ketu | Ke | के | Dragon's tail |
| Ascendant | As, La, Asc | ल, लग्न | Lagna |

Markers: (R), व, वक्री = retrograde · (C), अ, अस्त = combust · ↑, उच्च = exalted · ↓, नीच =
debilitated · मार्गी = direct · स्व = own sign · मि = friend's sign · श = enemy's sign (context!)

Signs: 1 Mesh (Aries), 2 Vrishabh (Taurus), 3 Mithun (Gemini), 4 Kark (Cancer), 5 Simha (Leo),
6 Kanya (Virgo), 7 Tula (Libra), 8 Vrishchik (Scorpio), 9 Dhanu (Sagittarius), 10 Makar
(Capricorn), 11 Kumbh (Aquarius), 12 Meen (Pisces).

Degrees: "Ma 24-18-40" = 24°18'40" in its sign. "Ma 54.31" = absolute longitude (Taurus 24.31°).
Dates: Indian reports use DD-MM-YYYY. Dasha tables may list only end dates.

## 4. Handwritten janampatri

- Usually Devanagari, Vikram Samvat dates (Samvat = Gregorian year + 56/57) and ghati-pal time
  (1 ghati = 24 minutes, 1 pal = 24 seconds) counted from sunrise ("इष्ट काल"). Convert: birth time =
  sunrise + ghati x 24 min. If both Samvat and Gregorian dates appear, trust the Gregorian one and
  check the weekday matches.
- The chart is almost always North Indian style. Planet abbreviations are single Devanagari letters.
- Tithi, nakshatra and vara written on the patri are a great check: recompute them and compare.
- If any digit is ambiguous (1/7, 4/9 in Devanagari ४/९), ask rather than guess.

## 5. Time and place pitfalls

- **Indian time zone history** (`Asia/Kolkata` in the script handles these):
  - Before 1906 (and in Bombay/Calcutta city time until the 1940s-50s) local times were used;
    tzdata applies Madras time (UTC+5:21:10) before 1906. If the report says "LMT", convert with
    the birth longitude (LMT = UTC + longitude/15 hours) and pass a fixed offset.
  - **War time: 1 Oct 1941 - 14 May 1942 and 1 Sep 1942 - 14 Oct 1945 clocks ran at UTC+6:30**
    (an hour ahead of IST). A recorded clock time in that window is war time. Some families
    already "corrected" it to IST - ask if unsure, and show both charts if the Lagna changes.
- **Foreign births**: daylight saving time. Use the IANA zone (`America/New_York`,
  `Europe/London`) so the script applies the right offset for that date.
- **Before sunrise**: in Vedic reckoning the weekday changes at sunrise, so a 4 am Tuesday birth is
  a Monday "vara". The script handles it; mention it when the person relies on the weekday.
- **AM/PM**: "12:30 night" is 00:30 of the NEXT civil date. Confirm odd hours.
- **Coordinates**: 0.1 degree of error moves the Lagna by only a few arc-minutes, so the nearest
  town is fine. Hemisphere signs matter (south/west are negative).

## 7. Reconciliation checklist

1. Lagna sign and degree within ~1 degree? If not: time zone, AM/PM, ayanamsa, place.
2. Moon sign and nakshatra-pada identical? A mismatch moves every dasha date.
3. All planets in the same signs? Fast planets near a sign edge may differ by software.
4. Dasha dates within a few days? A ~1 year shift = different Moon degree (time error).
5. Report says "KP" or "Krishnamurti"? Rerun with `--ayanamsa kp` to compare cusps.
6. Report looks tropical (Sun in Aries on 10 April births, no ayanamsa line)? It is a Western
   chart; recompute sidereally and explain the difference (~24 degrees).
7. Write down which data you finally used, and why, in the reading.
