# Life areas: what to examine and how to time it

For each area: promise factors -> varga -> Jaimini/KP check -> timing triggers. Rate promise
(strong / moderate / weak) and give timing windows from the dasha + transit tables.

## Career and profession
- Houses: 10th (career, status), 6th (service, daily work, competition), 7th (business,
  partnerships), 2nd and 11th (income), 3rd (self-effort, media), 9th (fortune, mentors).
- Planets: 10th lord's sign/house/nakshatra; planets in 10th; Sun (authority, government),
  Saturn (service, masses, industry), Mercury (business, IT, finance, writing), Jupiter (teaching,
  law, finance, advisory), Mars (engineering, police/army, surgery, sports, real estate), Venus
  (arts, media, fashion, hospitality, luxury), Moon (public-facing, hospitality, care), Rahu
  (technology, foreign, unconventional), Ketu (research, coding, spiritual, healing).
- Varga: **D10** - D10 Lagna and its lord, 10th of D10, planets' D10 dignity.
- Jaimini: Amatyakaraka (career significator), A10 (Rajyapada), 10th from Arudha Lagna.
- KP: 10th cusp sub-lord signifying 2, 6, 10, 11 = job/success; 7 = business; 5, 8, 12 = breaks.
- **Government job** tendencies: strong Sun connected with 10th/10th lord/6th; Sun in 10th or
  aspecting it; strong 10th lord with Sun; Jupiter influence for administration/teaching; Saturn
  for routine government service; D10 Sun strong. Exams also need the 4th/5th (education) and 6th
  (competition) strong in the running dasha. Give a "promise + best attempt windows" answer, not
  yes/no.
- Timing: dashas of 10th lord, planets in 10th, D10 Lagna lord, Amatyakaraka; Jupiter + Saturn
  double transit on 10th/10th lord; Saturn transiting 10th/11th from Moon; Sun transit over 10th
  for monthly peaks.

## Wealth and finances
- Houses: 2nd (savings), 11th (income, gains), 5th (speculation, investments), 9th (fortune),
  8th (inheritance, sudden money, debt), 12th (expenses), 4th (property, assets).
- Factors: Dhana yogas, strong 2nd/11th lords, Jupiter and Venus strength, D2 (Hora: planets in
  Leo hora = self-earned/active, Cancer hora = inherited/passive in common interpretation),
  SAV of 11th vs 12th (11th > 12th favours saving), benefics in 2nd/11th from Arudha Lagna.
- Timing: dashas of 2nd/11th lords and Dhana yoga planets; Jupiter transiting 2nd/11th from Moon
  or Lagna. Never give investment or trading signals; say "financial advisor se salah lein".

## Marriage and relationships
- Houses: 7th (spouse), 2nd (family life), 11th (fulfilment, friendship), 5th (romance), 8th
  (marital longevity, in-laws), 12th (bed pleasures).
- Planets: 7th lord, planets in/aspecting 7th, Venus (all charts), Jupiter (traditionally the
  husband significator in a woman's chart), Moon (emotional compatibility).
- Varga: **D9** - D9 Lagna, D9 7th house and lord, Venus and 7th lord in D9.
- Jaimini: Darakaraka, Upapada (UL) and its 2nd house, A7.
- KP: 7th cusp sub-lord signifying 2, 7, 11 = marriage promised; 1, 6, 10 (denial houses) show
  delays/obstacles.
- Delay indicators (not denial): Saturn on 7th/7th lord/Venus, 7th lord in dusthana, Venus
  combust/debilitated without cancellation, Rahu/Ketu on 1-7 axis. Say "later rather than early",
  with windows.
- Timing: dashas/ADs of 7th lord, Venus, planets in 7th, D9 Lagna lord, Darakaraka, planets
  connected to 2-7-11; Jupiter transiting 7th, 1st, 5th, 9th from Lagna/Moon or aspecting the 7th
  lord; double transit on 7th; age-appropriate windows. Give 1-3 windows ranked.
- Compatibility (if asked): Ashtakoota (36 points: varna 1, vashya 2, tara 3, yoni 4, graha maitri
  5, gana 6, bhakoot 7, nadi 8) is a starting point; also compare 7th houses, Venus/Mars, D9,
  dasha harmony in the coming years, and Manglik with cancellations. Score alone never decides.

## Children
- 5th house, 5th lord, Jupiter (putrakaraka), D7 (Saptamsa), Putrakaraka (Jaimini), KP 5th cusp
  sub-lord in 2, 5, 11.
- Timing: dashas of 5th lord/Jupiter/planets in 5th; Jupiter transit over 5th, 5th lord, or natal
  Jupiter; double transit on 5th.
- Never predict infertility, the child's sex, or miscarriage. If delays show: "medical guidance
  ke saath", gentle tone.

## Health
- Lagna, Lagna lord, Sun (vitality), Moon (mind), 6th (acute illness), 8th (chronic, surgery),
  12th (hospital), maraka periods, D30 (Trimsamsa) for vulnerabilities, planets in 6/8 from Lagna.
- Body map by sign/house: 1 head, 2 face/throat, 3 shoulders/arms/lungs, 4 chest/heart(emotional),
  5 stomach/upper abdomen/heart, 6 intestines, 7 kidneys/lower back, 8 genitals/excretory, 9 hips/
  thighs, 10 knees, 11 calves/ankles, 12 feet.
- Phrase as "is area ka dhyan rakhein, regular check-up karvayein". No diagnoses, no claims about
  treatment outcome, no death/longevity, always "doctor se salah lein".

## Education
- 4th (formal schooling, degrees), 5th (intelligence, exams), 2nd (early learning), 9th (higher
  education, PhD), Mercury and Jupiter, D24 (Chaturvimsamsa). Subjects: Mercury - commerce, IT,
  math; Jupiter - law, finance, teaching; Mars - engineering; Sun/Moon - medicine with 6/8/12 links;
  Venus - arts, design; Saturn - technical/administrative; Rahu - technology, foreign education.
- Timing: dashas of 4th/5th/9th lords, Mercury/Jupiter; Jupiter transit over 4th/5th.

## Property and vehicles
- 4th house and lord, Mars (land), Venus (vehicles, comforts), Saturn (old/large property), Moon
  (home), D4 (Chaturthamsa), D16 (vehicles), KP 4th cusp in 4, 11, 12 (purchase with loan).
- Timing: 4th lord / Mars / Venus periods; Jupiter-Saturn influence on 4th.

## Foreign travel and settlement
- 12th (foreign residence), 9th (long journeys), 7th (living away), 3rd (short trips), Rahu,
  movable/water signs, 12th lord in 9th/7th or vice versa, Lagna lord in 12th. KP 12th cusp in 3,
  9, 12.
- Timing: Rahu periods, 12th/9th lord periods, Saturn/Rahu transit over 12th/9th.

## Litigation, competition, debts
- 6th house (contests, debts), 8th (obstacles), 12th (losses), Mars and Saturn, 6th lord; upachaya
  malefics help win. Suggest a lawyer for decisions.

## Family: parents and siblings
- Mother: 4th, Moon, D12. Father: 9th (and 10th in North Indian practice), Sun, D12. Younger
  siblings: 3rd, Mars, D3. Elder siblings: 11th. Spouse's family: 8th / 2nd from 7th.
- Keep predictions about relatives gentle and general.

## Spirituality and inner life
- 9th (dharma), 5th (mantra), 12th (moksha), 8th (occult, deep research), Ketu, Jupiter,
  Karakamsha and 12th from it, D20. Moon-Ketu/Jupiter-Ketu/Saturn-Ketu combinations.
