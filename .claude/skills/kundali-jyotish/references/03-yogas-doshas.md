# Yogas and doshas: how to judge them

Contents: 1. When a yoga actually works · 2. Major yogas · 3. Doshas with cancellations ·
4. Sade Sati and Saturn's dhaiya · 5. Wording

## 1. When a yoga actually works

The script lists rule-based *candidates*. Before you name a yoga in a reading, check:
1. **Strength of participants** - dignity in D1 and D9, not combust/debilitated without
   cancellation, not in planetary war, decent SAV of the houses involved.
2. **House context** - yogas in kendra/trikona deliver; the same yoga in 6/8/12 is diluted or
   delivers through struggle.
3. **Dasha** - a yoga fructifies in the dashas/antardashas of its planets (or of planets in their
   nakshatras). Say *when* it is likely to show up.
4. **Afflictions** - malefic aspects, Rahu/Ketu conjunction, enemy signs reduce results.
Grade each: strong / moderate / weak / nominal. Many charts have 10+ named yogas; only the strong
ones deserve emphasis. Ten "yogas" that never deliver make a reading feel fake.

## 2. Major yogas

| Yoga | Formation | Results when strong | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Raj yoga (Parashari) | lords of a kendra and a trikona conjoin, mutually aspect or exchange signs | status, authority, rise | strongest when the planets are a yogakaraka or Lagna lord; check D10 |
| Yogakaraka planet | one planet owns kendra + trikona (e.g. Saturn for Taurus/Libra, Mars for Cancer/Leo, Venus for Capricorn/Aquarius) | its dasha is a high point if it is strong | read its placement carefully |
| Dhana yoga | lords of 2 and 11 associate with lords of 1, 5, 9 (or each other) | wealth accumulation | confirm with D2, SAV of 2/11 |
| Pancha Mahapurusha | Mars (Ruchaka), Mercury (Bhadra), Jupiter (Hamsa), Venus (Malavya), Saturn (Shasha) in own/exalted sign in a kendra from Lagna | outstanding qualities of that planet | from Moon is weaker; combustion spoils it |
| Gajakesari | Jupiter in kendra from Moon | wisdom, reputation, support | very common (~1 in 3 charts); weak if Jupiter is debilitated/combust/in dusthana from Lagna |
| Budha-Aditya | Sun + Mercury together | intelligence, communication | extremely common; meaningful only in a good house with Mercury not deeply combust |
| Chandra-Mangala | Moon + Mars together/opposite | earning drive, business instinct | emotional heat |
| Adhi yoga | benefics in 6, 7, 8 from Moon | leadership, comfort | partial if only one or two |
| Amala | natural benefic in 10th from Lagna/Moon | good reputation | |
| Sunapha / Anapha / Durudhara | planets (not Sun/nodes) in 2nd / 12th / both from Moon | self-made wealth / personality / comfort | absence = Kemadruma |
| Viparita Raj yoga | lord of 6, 8 or 12 placed in 6, 8 or 12 (Harsha, Sarala, Vimala) | gains after/through adversity | needs the planet unafflicted |
| Neecha Bhanga Raj yoga | a debilitated planet's fall is cancelled (see section 3) | rise after early struggle | quality varies widely |
| Parivartana | two planets in each other's signs | links the two houses' affairs | Maha (good houses), Khala (3rd), Dainya (6/8/12) |
| Lakshmi yoga | strong 9th lord in kendra/trikona in own/exalted sign + strong Lagna lord | fortune, grace | |
| Saraswati yoga | Jupiter, Venus, Mercury in kendra/trikona/2nd, Jupiter strong | learning, arts | |
| Vipreet/Kartari | Shubha kartari: benefics on both sides of a house/planet (protection); Papa kartari: malefics on both sides (pressure) | | check for Lagna and Moon |

Jaimini indicators: Atmakaraka's sign in D9 (Karakamsha) shows the soul's direction; planets in
the 10th from Arudha Lagna (AL) show public status; benefics in 2nd/11th from AL show sustained
wealth; Upapada (UL) and its 2nd show marriage durability; AK + Amatyakaraka connection is a
Jaimini Raj yoga.

## 3. Doshas, with their cancellations

**Manglik / Kuja dosha.** Mars in 1, 2, 4, 7, 8 or 12 from Lagna (also checked from Moon and
Venus). It is very common - a large share of all charts qualify from at least one reference point.
Common cancellations (tradition-dependent; cite them as "traditionally considered"): Mars in own
sign (Aries, Scorpio) or exalted (Capricorn); Mars conjunct or aspected by Jupiter; Mars in
Cancer/Leo for some houses; Lagna Aries/Scorpio/Cancer/Leo per some authors; both partners
Manglik; "dosha reduces after 28" (folk). Weight: one factor in compatibility among many (7th
house, Venus, D9, Upapada, mutual dasha harmony). Never say "marriage will fail"; say what Mars
indicates (strong will, need for respectful conflict style, physical energy) and how to handle it.
Report pages that declare "Manglik" from the Lagna alone should be qualified.

**Kaal Sarp.** All seven planets on one side of the Rahu-Ketu axis. Not described in BPHS or other
classical texts; a modern popular concept. Many accomplished people have it. If asked, explain
honestly, describe the nodes' actual houses (themes of obsession vs detachment), and do not
recommend costly rituals. Partial versions (a planet outside the axis) are even weaker claims.

**Kemadruma.** No planets (excluding Sun, Rahu, Ketu) in 2nd and 12th from Moon. Cancelled by:
planet conjunct Moon, planets in kendra from Lagna or from Moon, Moon in kendra aspected by
Jupiter, strong Moon. Most charts that qualify are cancelled. When real: emotional self-reliance,
periods of feeling unsupported - pair it with how strong relationships/routines help.

**Neecha (debilitation) bhanga conditions** (any one helps; more = stronger):
lord of the debilitation sign in kendra from Lagna or Moon · lord of the planet's exaltation sign
in kendra from Lagna or Moon · the planet that is exalted in that sign is in kendra · debilitated
planet aspected by its dispositor · exchange with its dispositor · planet exalted in D9 ·
debilitated planet itself in a kendra (some authors).

**Guru-Chandal (Jupiter + Rahu)** - unconventional beliefs, teachers/advisors to vet carefully,
can also mean breakthrough learning. **Grahan (Sun/Moon with Rahu/Ketu)** - confidence (Sun) or
emotional (Moon) turbulence, helped by routine and supportive people. **Shrapit (Saturn + Rahu)**
- folk label; read as delays plus pressure in that house. **Pitru dosha** has several competing
definitions; if a report mentions it, say it is tradition-specific and suggest simple ancestral
remembrance (shraddha, daan) only if the person values that.

**Gandanta / Gandmool** - Moon or Lagna at water-fire junction degrees; traditional shanti is
optional. Describe as "sensitive start, needs nurturing", never as danger.

## 4. Sade Sati and Saturn's dhaiya

- **Sade Sati**: Saturn transiting the 12th, 1st and 2nd sign from the natal Moon (~7.5 years).
  Phase 1 (12th): expenses, sleep, relocation, endings. Phase 2 (over Moon): mental pressure,
  responsibility, reality checks, maturity. Phase 3 (2nd): family/finance restructuring, speech.
- **Dhaiya (small panoti)**: Saturn in 4th (Kantaka - home, comfort, mother, property stress) or
  8th (Ashtama - obstacles, health vigilance, sudden changes) from Moon, ~2.5 years each.
- **How bad is it?** Depends on: Saturn's functional nature for the Lagna (yogakaraka for
  Taurus/Libra, Lagna lord for Capricorn/Aquarius - often productive), Saturn's natal strength,
  Saturn's own BAV bindus in the transited signs (≥4 = manageable/good, ≤2 = harder), SAV of those
  signs, and the running dasha. Many people build careers during Sade Sati. Never present it as a
  disaster; present it as a demanding, character-building phase with dates and coping steps.
- Dates come from the script's Sade Sati table (it includes retrograde re-entries). Say which
  phase is running and when it ends.

## 5. Wording

- "Aapki kundli mein Gajakesari yoga hai, lekin Guru 8th house mein hai, isliye iska asar
  moderate hai - padhai aur mentors se laabh, par effort zaroori."
- "Report mein Manglik likha hai; Lagna se Mangal 7th mein hai, par Mangal par Guru ki drishti
  hai jo paramparik roop se ise kam maanti hai. Shaadi ke liye dono kundliyon ka poora milan
  dekhna sahi rahega - sirf is ek baat se faisla na karein."
- Avoid: "dosh", "shraap", "barbaadi", "naash" as verdicts. Use "challenge", "dhyan dene wali baat".
