## SCRIPT

### [0:00] COLD OPEN
**[VISUAL: B/W archive photo, 1920s New York press conference. Text: "30 OCTOBER 1924"]**

30 October 1924. New York. Ek press conference chal rahi hai, aur reporters ke saamne ek aadmi
khada hai, naam Thomas Midgley. Uske saamne ek bottle rakhi hai. Bottle mein ek liquid hai jisne pichhle
kuch dinon mein New Jersey ki ek refinery mein kaam karne wale kuch mazdooron ki jaan le li thi.
Akhbaar usse "loony gas" bula rahe the, yaani "paagal karne wali gas". **[SRC: Bayway refinery deaths, Oct 1924; Kitman, *The Nation* 2000] [VERIFY count]**

Aur yeh aadmi kya karta hai? Reporters ke saamne woh liquid apne haathon par malta hai. Phir bottle
naak ke paas laake lagbhag ek minute tak soonghta hai. Aur muskura ke kehta hai: "Dekho, kuch nahi hua."
**[VISUAL: illustration, haath par liquid, slow zoom]**

Jo baat us din reporters ko nahi pata thi: kuch hi mahine pehle yahi aadmi isi cheez ke zeher se itna
bimaar ho gaya tha ki use kaam chhod ke aaraam karne jaana pada tha. **[VERIFY]**

### [0:45] STAKES + PROMISE
Ab aap sochoge: 100 saal purani kahani, mujhe kya? Toh suniye. Is liquid ka bill sirf America ne nahi
bhara. Ek research ke hisaab se sirf America mein logon ke **800 million se zyada IQ points** iski wajah
se gaye. **[SRC: McFarland et al., PNAS 2022]** Aur yeh zeher aaj bhi Bharat ke karodon bachchon ke
khoon mein hai, kuch shehron mein shayad aapki rasoi ki **haldi** tak mein.

Last tak sunna. Is video ke baad aap petrol pump, battery wale ki dukaan aur apne masale ke dabbe ko
pehle jaisi nazar se nahi dekhoge. Aur end mein ek twist hai: isi aadmi ki ek aur invention, jo isse bhi badi thi.

### [1:30] FOUNDATION: Lead dimaag ke saath karta kya hai?
**[VISUAL: periodic table, "Pb" highlight → simple brain graphic]**

Pehle ek cheez samajhna zaroori hai. Lead, yaani seesa, ek heavy metal hai. Hamare shareer ko uski
koi zaroorat nahi hai. Par problem yeh hai ki lead shareer mein **calcium ka bhes badal ke** ghus jaata hai.

Simple misaal: jaise koi chor nakli ID card dikha ke building mein ghus jaaye. Guard ko lagta hai
apna aadmi hai. Dimaag ke andar, nerves ke beech jo signal jaate hain, unmein calcium ka bada role hai.
Lead wahan baith ke signal gadbad kar deta hai.

Aur sabse bura asar kis par? **Bachchon par.** Kyunki unka dimaag ban raha hota hai, aur unka shareer
lead ko zyada absorb karta hai. Isliye WHO aur CDC dono kehte hain ki bachchon ke liye lead ka koi
"safe level" hai hi nahi. **[SRC: WHO lead fact sheet; CDC] [VERIFY wording]**

Yaad rakhna: **koi safe level nahi.** Aage yeh line kaam aayegi.

### [4:30] STORY 1: Engine ki "khat-khat" aur ek patent
**[VISUAL: 1920s car, engine animation, text "ENGINE KNOCK"]**

1920s mein America mein cars tezi se badh rahi thi. Par ek problem thi: engine mein **"knocking"**,
ek khat-khat ki awaaz. Fuel galat time par jal jaata tha, engine ki power kam hoti thi, aur engine kharab hota tha.

General Motors ki research team, Charles Kettering aur unke engineer Thomas Midgley, saalon tak isse
door karne wali cheez dhoondhte rahe. Aur December 1921 mein mili: **tetraethyl lead**. Petrol mein
thoda sa milao, aur knocking gayab. **[SRC: GM research history] [VERIFY date]**

Ab yahan pehla twist hai, aur yahi is video ka "asli bill" hai. Knocking rokne ka ek aur tareeka us
waqt bhi pata tha: **ethanol**, yaani alcohol, jo makka ya ganne se banta hai. Par ethanol koi bhi
bana sakta tha, uska patent nahi ho sakta tha. Lead wala formula patent ho sakta tha. Matlab har
gallon petrol par royalty. **[SRC: Jamie Lincoln Kitman, "The Secret History of Lead", *The Nation*, 2000] [VERIFY]**

Aur jab yeh product bazaar mein aaya, toh uske naam se ek shabd gayab tha. Naam rakha gaya **"Ethyl"**.
"Lead" kahin nahi likha tha. **[VERIFY]**

Feb 1923 mein Dayton, Ohio ke ek petrol pump par pehli baar "Ethyl" petrol bika. **[VERIFY]**

### [7:00] STORY 2: "Loony gas"
**[VISUAL: newspaper headline style card "LOONY GAS"]**

Phir aaya October 1924. New Jersey mein Standard Oil ki Bayway refinery, jahan yeh additive banta tha.
Wahan mazdoor ajeeb harkatein karne lage: hallucinations, daure, behoshi. Kuch ki maut ho gayi, aur
dozens hospital pahunche. **[SRC: Kitman 2000; contemporary NYT coverage] [VERIFY numbers]**

Hungama hua. Kuch shehron ne isko kuch time ke liye ban bhi kiya. Aur tab hui wahi press conference
jo humne shuru mein dekhi. Midgley ka message tha: "Galti mazdooron ki hai, product ki nahi."

Industry ka argument simple tha: "Thoda sa lead hai, khule mein ud jaayega, koi nuksaan nahi."
Aur iske baad agle **40 saal** tak, jo bhi research hui, uska bada hissa industry ke funded scientists
ne kiya, jinka kehna tha ki jab tak nuksaan **sabit** na ho, tab tak sab safe hai. **[SRC: "Kehoe paradigm", Robert Kehoe] [VERIFY]**

Ek line mein: *"Saboot laao ki zeher hai", na ki "saboot laao ki safe hai".*

### [9:00] STORY 3: Ek scientist jo dharti ki umar naapne nikla tha
**[VISUAL: Clair Patterson photo, lab, Earth graphic, "4.55 BILLION YEARS"]**

Ab ek doosre insaan ki kahani. **Is insaan ka naam yaad rakhna: Clair Patterson.**

1950s mein Patterson ka kaam tha dharti ki umar naapna. Iske liye usse rocks mein lead ke bahut
chhote-chhote amounts naapne the. Par ek problem aayi: uske lab ke har sample mein **lead hi lead** tha.
Hawa mein, paani mein, kaanch ke bartanon mein, haathon par.

Usne duniya ka ek super-clean lab banaya, aur dharti ki umar nikaal di: **lagbhag 4.55 billion saal**.
Woh number aaj bhi textbooks mein hai. **[SRC: Patterson 1956, *Geochimica et Cosmochimica Acta*]**

Par asli bomb uske baad phata. Patterson ne socha: agar mera lab itna lead se bhara hai, toh insaan ka
kya haal hoga? Usne samundar ki parton aur Greenland ki barf ki parton mein dekha. Purani barf mein
lead bahut kam tha, aur pichhle kuch dashakon ki barf mein kai guna zyada. **[SRC: Patterson 1965, "Contaminated and Natural Lead Environments of Man"] [VERIFY details]**

Matlab yeh lead "natural" nahi tha. **Yeh petrol se aa raha tha.**

Patterson ne awaaz uthayi. Bataya jaata hai ki uski kuch funding band hui aur usse industry-friendly
panels se door rakha gaya. **[VERIFY]** Par woh ruka nahi. Aakhir mein America mein 1970s se petrol
mein lead kam hona shuru hua, aur 1996 mein road vehicles ke liye ban laga. **[SRC: US EPA] [VERIFY]**

Aur poori duniya mein? Leaded petrol bechne wala aakhri desh tha **Algeria, 2021 mein**. UN ne
announce kiya: duniya se leaded petrol khatam. **[SRC: UNEP, Aug 2021]**

Socho: ek cheez jo 1921 mein shuru hui, uske khatam hone mein **100 saal** lage.

### [11:30] BILL: 824 million IQ points
**[VISUAL: data card "824,000,000 IQ POINTS · 170M+ Americans · PNAS 2022"]**

2022 mein researchers ne hisaab lagaya ki leaded petrol ki wajah se America mein bachpan mein kitna
lead exposure hua, aur uska dimaag par kya asar pada. Unka estimate: aaj zinda **17 crore se zyada
Americans** ke milake **lagbhag 824 million IQ points** kam hue. Average dekho toh har insaan ke
lagbhag 2–3 points, aur 1960s mein paida hue logon ke liye usse kaafi zyada. **[SRC: McFarland, Hauer & Reuben, PNAS 2022, doi:10.1073/pnas.2118631119]**

2–3 points sunne mein chhote lagte hain. Par jab poori population ka graph thoda sa left shift hota
hai, toh "kamzor" side par khade logon ki ginti bahut badh jaati hai.

### [12:30] DOOSRI SIDE ⭐
Ab fair rehte hain, kyunki is topic par internet par bahut kuch over-the-top bhi bola jaata hai.

1. **"Lead ki wajah se crime badha"** wali theory bahut viral hai. Kuch studies ne correlation dikhaya,
   par baad ke meta-analyses kehte hain ki asar shayad chhota hai aur publication bias bhi hai.
   **Toh yeh "sabit fact" nahi, ek debated hypothesis hai.** **[SRC: Higney, Hanley & Moro 2022 meta-analysis] [VERIFY]**
2. **Yeh kahani sirf villain ki nahi, jeet ki bhi hai.** Duniya ne milke ek global zeher hata diya.
   Matlab insaan galti theek kar sakta hai.
3. **Panic mat karo.** Lead exposure ka matlab har insaan ka nuksaan nahi. Risk sabse zyada bachchon aur
   kuch khaas sources ke paas rehne walon ko hai.

### [13:30] BHARAT: kahani khatam nahi hui
**[VISUAL: India map + data card "~27.5 crore bachche · UNICEF & Pure Earth 2020"]**

Bharat ne bhi petrol se lead hataya, lagbhag saal 2000 tak. **[VERIFY exact date]** Toh kya sab theek
ho gaya? 2020 mein UNICEF aur Pure Earth ki report aayi, *"The Toxic Truth"*. Duniya ke lagbhag har 3 mein
se 1 bachche ke khoon mein lead "concern" wale level (5 µg/dL) par ya usse upar hai. Aur is list mein
sabse zyada bachche jis desh mein hain, woh hai **Bharat, lagbhag 27.5 crore**. **[SRC: UNICEF & Pure Earth, *The Toxic Truth*, 2020] [VERIFY: modelled estimate hai, yeh bolna]**

Petrol nahi, toh ab kahan se? Teen bade raaste:

**1. Battery recycling.** Purani gaadi / inverter ki lead-acid batteries ko kai jagah bina safety ke,
khule mein toda aur pighlaya jaata hai. Dhuaan aur dhool paas ke gharon tak jaati hai. **[SRC: Toxic Truth report]**

**2. Haldi.** 2024 mein ek Stanford-led study ne South Asia ke 23 shehron se haldi ke sample liye.
Zyada tar sample theek the, par kuch shehron mein lead bahut zyada mila. Bihar ke **Patna** samet kuch
shehron mein 1,000 µg/g se bhi zyada. Kyun? Kyunki kuch log haldi ko chamakdaar peela dikhane ke liye
**lead chromate**, yaani ek industrial peela paint pigment, milate hain. Chamakdaar haldi = zyada daam.
**[SRC: Forsyth et al. 2024, *Science of the Total Environment*; [Stanford FSI summary](https://fsi.stanford.edu/node/36768)]**

**[VISUAL: do haldi bowls, ek normal, ek neon-peeli. Text "CHAMAK = DAAM?"]**

**3. Kuch cosmetics aur kuch dawaiyan.** Kuch sindoor samples aur kuch bina testing wali traditional
dawaiyon mein bhi heavy metals mile hain. **Har product mein nahi.** Isliye tested, certified product lo,
aur kisi bhi dawai ko doctor se poochhe bina mat lo. **[SRC: Saper et al., JAMA 2008] [VERIFY]**

### [16:00] ACHHI KHABAR: Bangladesh ne kar dikhaya
Bangladesh mein researchers ne yahi haldi wala source pakda. Phir sarkar, food safety officers aur
media ne milke 2019 ke aas-paas campaign chalaya: mills par raid, jurmana, aur bazaaron mein awareness.
Kuch hi saalon mein bazaar ki haldi mein lead adulteration lagbhag khatam ho gaya, aur workers ke
khoon mein lead bhi kam hua. **[SRC: Forsyth et al. 2023, *Environmental Research*] [VERIFY exact %]**

Matlab yeh problem **solve ho sakti hai**. Bas pehle maanna padta hai ki problem hai.

### [16:45] PAYOFF: Thomas Midgley ki doosri invention
Ab wapas Thomas Midgley par, woh aadmi jo haath par lead mal ke muskura raha tha. Usne sirf leaded
petrol nahi banaya. Kuch saal baad usne fridge aur AC ke liye ek "bilkul safe" gas banayi: **CFC**,
yaani Freon.

Aur 50 saal baad pata chala ki yahi CFCs dharti ki **ozone layer mein chhed** kar rahe the. **[SRC: Molina & Rowland 1974; Montreal Protocol 1987]**

Ek aadmi, do inventions, dono "genius", aur dono ne poori dharti par asar daala. Ek environmental
historian ne likha ki Midgley ne dharti ke atmosphere par **itihaas ke kisi bhi akele jeev se zyada asar**
daala. **[SRC: J.R. McNeill, *Something New Under the Sun*, 2000]**

Aur unki maut? 1940 mein unhe polio hua. Bed se uthne ke liye unhone ek rassi aur pulley ka system
banaya. 1944 mein woh usi system mein uljhe hue mile. **[VERIFY: kuch sources ise suicide maante hain, voice-over mein yeh bolo]**

### [17:30] AAPKE LIYE (3 kaam)
1. **Haldi aur masale:** jaane-maane, sealed, FSSAI-licensed brands lo, khaaskar bachchon ke khaane ke
   liye. Khule mein bikne wali "neon" chamakdaar haldi se bacho. Ghar par paani mein daal ke "lead test"
   wale viral jugaad par bharosa mat karo, woh reliable nahi hain. **[VERIFY FSSAI guidance]**
2. **Batteries:** purani battery sirf authorised dealer ko wapas do (Battery Waste Management Rules 2022).
   Ghar mein koi battery / metal ka kaam karta hai toh ghar aane se pehle kapde badle aur haath dhoye. **[VERIFY]**
3. **Bachche:** agar aap kisi battery recycling / smelting area ke paas rehte ho, ya bachche mein
   development ko lekar chinta hai, toh **doctor se blood lead test ke baare mein poochho**. Khud se koi ilaaj nahi.

*(On-screen: "Yeh medical advice nahi hai. Doctor se salah lein.")*

### [18:15] CLOSE
Ek aadmi ne patent ke chakkar mein 100 saal tak duniya ki hawa mein zeher milaya. Ek doosre aadmi ne
dharti ki umar naapte-naapte use pakad liya. Aur aaj woh kahani hamari rasoi tak aa pahunchi hai.

Ab aap batao: **kya FSSAI ko har saal masalon ki testing ke results public karne chahiye? Haan ya na,
aur kyun?** Comment mein likho, main padhta hoon.
