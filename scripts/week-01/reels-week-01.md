## R5: "Jo kabhi-kabhi reply karta hai, uski aadat kyun lagti hai?" (60 sec)
**Pillar:** 🧠 Dimaag ka Jaal · **Cover text:** "KABHI-KABHI REPLY = ADDICTION?"

| Time | Voice | Visual | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–4s | "Jo insaan hamesha reply kare, woh boring lagta hai. Jo kabhi-kabhi kare, uski aadat lag jaati hai. Kyun?" | Phone with "seen" ticks | – |
| 4–20s | "1950s mein psychologist B.F. Skinner ne kabootaron par experiment kiya. Jab daana **har baar** milta tha, kabootar normal rehte the. Jab daana **kabhi-kabhi, random** milta tha, woh lever sabse zyada dabate the, aur daana band hone ke baad bhi lambe time tak dabate rehte the." | Pigeon + lever animation | Ferster & Skinner 1957 |

| 35–50s | "Rishton mein researchers ne isse 'traumatic bonding' se joda hai: achhe aur bure ka random cycle insaan ko aur zyada baandh deta hai." | Text card | Dutton & Painter 1981 |
| 50–60s | "Toh agli baar kisi ke reply ka intezaar dil mein khalbali machaye, poochho: yeh pyaar hai, ya pattern? Aap batao, aapne kabhi yeh feel kiya? 👇" | Face | – |
