# Example (Long-form): "UPI free hai, toh paisa kaun kama raha hai?"

> **Purpose:** yeh dikhana ki playbook ko ek original topic par kaise lagate hain.
> `[VERIFY]` wale har number ko record karne se pehle latest official source se check karo.
> Yeh numbers memory se hain aur purane ho sakte hain.

## 0. Planning

- **Titles:**
  1. "UPI free hai, phir bhi PhonePe aur Google Pay kaise kama rahe hain?"
  2. "₹0 fee ka asli bill: UPI ka chhupa business"
  3. "Aapka har UPI payment kisi aur ki kamaai hai. Kaise?"
- **Thumbnail:** chehra (shak wala expression) + phone par "₹0" + red text "ASLI BILL?"
- **Core question:** Jab UPI payment par na aap fee dete ho na dukaandaar, toh yeh system
  chal kaise raha hai, aur fayda kise ho raha hai?
- **Bold thesis:** "Free cheez ka bill hamesha kahin aur chukaaya jaata hai. UPI mein yeh bill
  teen jagah se jaata hai: aapka tax, aapka data, aur aapka bharosa."
- **Value promise:** video ke end tak aap samjhoge ki payment app aapko loan aur insurance kyun
  bechti hai, aur kab "haan" bolna nuksaan hai.
- **Foil:** "UPI free hai kyunki government ne banaya hai. Bas, kahani khatam."
- **Steelman:** zero-fee ne UPI ko duniya ka sabse bada real-time payment system banaya,
  aur chhote dukaandaar digital economy mein aaye. Yeh asli fayda hai.
- **Length:** ~14 min (~2,000 words)

### CHAPTER 1: Bill #1, aapka tax (~3 min)
- **Chapter question:** zero fee ka kharcha kaun uthaata hai?
- **Story:** 2016 launch, 2019–20 mein MDR zero karne ka faisla, "digital India" push.
- **Evidence:** zero-MDR notification [SRC]; chhote merchant transactions par govt incentive
  scheme, saal aur amount [SRC: PIB, VERIFY].
- **Iska matlab:** "Fee aapki jeb se seedhe nahi jaati, par budget se jaati hai, aur budget
  aapke tax se banta hai."
- **Mini-reveal:** banks ke liye bhi UPI chalana sasta nahi. Servers, fraud checks, failed
  transactions, sab ka kharcha hai [SRC: industry body statements, VERIFY].
- **Next loop:** "Par agar fee nahi milti, toh private companies itna paisa laga kyun rahi hain?"

### CHAPTER 2: Bill #2, aapka data (~4 min)
- **Chapter question:** PhonePe aur Google Pay asli kamaai kahan se karte hain?
- **Story:** payment ko "dukaan ka darwaza" ki tarah samjhao. Aap roz aate ho, isliye unhe
  pata hai aap kya khareedte ho, kab, kitna.
- **Evidence:** revenue lines: lending distribution, insurance, mutual funds / broking,
  soundbox rental, ads / app store [SRC: company filings, VERIFY].
- **Iska matlab:** "Payment product nahi hai. Payment **chaara** hai. Asli product loan aur
  insurance hai, aur unka customer aap ho."
- **Mini-reveal:** jitne zyada payments, utna behtar aapka "risk profile". Isi se
  "pre-approved" offers aate hain.
- **Next loop:** "Par jab sirf do companies ke paas sabka data aur sabki aadat ho, tab kya hota hai?"

### CHAPTER 3: Bill #3, aapka bharosa / concentration risk (~3 min)
- **Chapter question:** do companies ke paas ~80% market hona problem kyun hai?
- **Story:** NPCI ka 30% cap ka rule, aur uski deadline baar-baar kyun badhti hai.
- **Evidence:** NPCI circulars, deadline extensions [SRC, VERIFY].
- **Iska matlab:** agar ek app down ho jaaye toh aadha desh ruk sakta hai. Aur bargaining power
  unke paas hai, kal fee aayi toh?
- **Mini-reveal:** dono badi apps ke maalik / investors videshi hain [SRC, VERIFY ownership
  structure], isliye "data sovereignty" ki bahas hoti hai.

### STEELMAN: "Free UPI ne India ko kya diya"
- **Unka strongest argument:** zero fee ki wajah se rehdi waale se lekar kirana tak sab digital
  hue. Cash ka kharcha ghata, record bana, aur chhote business ko credit history mili.
- **Kahan woh sahi hain:** adoption aur inclusion mein yeh duniya ka example hai.
- **Mera point:** fayda asli hai, par "free" ki keemat dikhni chahiye. Taaki hum loans, data aur
  concentration par soch-samajh ke faisle lein.

### PAYOFF (last 3 min)
- **Callback:** chai ke ₹10 wapas. "Ab dekho, is ek payment mein teen bill chhupe the."
- **Thesis dobara:** tax, data, bharosa, ab har ek ka evidence ke saath.
- **Aapke liye 3 actions:**
  1. "Pre-approved loan" dekh ke turant "haan" mat bolo. Interest rate aur processing fee
     doosre bank se compare karo.
  2. App permissions aur marketing consent check karo.
  3. Do UPI apps rakho. Ek down ho toh doosri chale (aur concentration bhi kam ho).

### CLOSE (last 30s)
- **Punch:** "UPI free nahi hai. Bas uska bill aapke haath mein nahi aata."
- **Comment sawaal:** "Kya UPI par ₹0.50 ki chhoti fee lagni chahiye agar usse system safe
  aur competitive bane? Haan ya na, comment mein reason ke saath."
- **Teaser:** "Agle video mein: 'Buy Now Pay Later'. Woh aur bhi bada khel hai."
