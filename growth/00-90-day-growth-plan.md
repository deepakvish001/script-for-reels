# 90-Day Growth Plan: YouTube (long-form) + Instagram Reels / YouTube Shorts

> **Imaandaar baat pehle:** koi bhi plan views ya subscribers ki **guarantee** nahi de sakta.
> Algorithm, timing aur thodi kismat bhi kaam karti hai. Jo control mein hai: **consistency, packaging,
> retention aur trust**. Yeh plan unhi par focus karta hai.

---

## 1. Positioning (aapka channel ek line mein)

> **"Duniya ke hidden systems, India ke nazariye se, receipts ke saath."**

| Rishabh-style | Aapka edge |
|---|---|
| Hidden-system lens, India-first, dramatic storytelling | Same lens + **har line ka source aur highlighted screenshot screen par** |
| Strong opinion | Strong opinion + **"doosri side" segment** |
| Science + spirituality "coincidences" | **Asli Indian science** (Madhava, iron pillar, wootz) jo bina stretch ke bhi amazing hai |
| Foil: rival creators | Foil: **myths, scams aur systems**, insaan nahi |

**Series names (brand banane ke liye):**
- 🔍 **"Asli Bill"**: free / sasti cheez ka asli kharcha kaun bharta hai
- 🧪 **"Bharat ka Asli Science"**: verified Indian science / history
- 🧠 **"Dimaag ka Jaal"**: psychology of persuasion, ads, apps, relationships
- 📰 **"Receipts"**: is hafte ki badi news ka hidden system, sources ke saath

## 2. Cadence (pakka rule)

| Platform | Kya | Kitna | 90 din mein |
|---|---|---|---|
| YouTube | Long-form, 10–15 min (mahine mein ek 18–22 min "flagship") | **3 / week**: Tue, Thu, Sat (Thu = flex; time kam ho toh skip → 2 / week) | 24–36 |
| Instagram Reels | 1:30–3:00 phone-selfie mini-explainer | **4 / week**: Mon, Wed, Fri, Sun (alternate din) | ~48 |
| YouTube Shorts | **Wahi reel**, same din cross-post (Shorts 3 min tak chal sakte hain) | 4 / week | ~48 |
| Aur kuch | **Kuch nahi.** No carousel, no story, no community post | – | – |

**Teen rules:**
1. **Har video ka content alag.** Reel kabhi long-form ka teaser, clip ya chhota version nahi hogi. Har
   reel aur har long-form ka topic alag. Registry: [`../scripts/INDEX.md`](../scripts/INDEX.md).
2. **Just-in-time.** Jis din post hai, research + script **usi din ya ek din pehle**. Pehle se banaya
   hua koi bhi draft use karna ho toh bhi D-1 par dobara research karo. Process:
   [`02-daily-build-system.md`](02-daily-build-system.md).
3. **Har factual line = link + highlighted screenshot.** Dono compulsory. Jis line ka screenshot nahi
   ban sakta, woh line script mein nahi jaayegi. Tool: [`../tools/proof/`](../tools/proof/).

## 4. Trend scan (har build se pehle, 15 min)

1. **Google Trends (India, past 24 h / 7 din)**: rising searches
2. **YouTube Trending + Explore** (News, Education)
3. **X / Instagram Explore**: kaunsa topic logon ko gussa / curious kar raha hai
4. **News sites**: The Hindu, Indian Express, Mint, ThePrint, Down To Earth, PIB, Reuters, BBC
5. **Reddit r/india**: logon ke asli sawaal
6. **Apne comments**: viewers ke sawaal = agle topics

**News-peg filter:** topic tabhi lo jab (a) 2 reliable sources ho, (b) aapka "hidden system" angle ho,
(c) har factual line ka screenshot-able source mile. Sirf outrage ke liye trend par mat koodo.

## 5. Month-by-month plan

### Month 1 (Week 1–4): Voice + rhythm
- 2 long-form / week (Tue + Sat), 4 reels / week. Thu tab add karo jab Tue aur Sat time par jaane lagein.
- Chaar pillars test karo: Asli Bill, Bharat ka Asli Science, Dimaag ka Jaal, Receipts
- Har reel ke 3 hook versions likho, shoot karo **sirf best wala**
- **Goal metrics** (follower count nahi):
  - Reels: 3-sec hold > 60%, avg watch > 50%
  - Long-form: CTR > 5%, first 30 sec retention > 70%, avg view duration > 35%

### Month 2 (Week 5–8): Double down
- 3 long-form / week. Jis pillar ka watch-time aur shares sabse zyada, uske 2× topics
- Jo reel topic sabse zyada chala, uska **naya angle** long-form mein (same content nahi, naya sawaal)
- Comment-to-content: top viewer sawaalon par reels

### Month 3 (Week 9–12): Scale + monetisation setup
- Series format pakka karo (same intro sting, same thumbnail style)
- Media kit (1 page): audience age, top videos, avg views, "receipts" positioning
- Brand rule: sirf relevant, legal, aapke audience ke kaam ke (upskilling, finance literacy, tools)
- YouTube Partner Program eligibility track karo **[VERIFY current thresholds on YouTube Help]**
- Ek "flagship" long-form (20 min) jo channel ka visiting card bane

## 6. Packaging rules

**Reel cover:** chehra + 3–4 shabd (bade) + ek object. Grid par series colour consistent.
**Reel first frame:** bolna 0.0 sec se. Text hook 4–7 shabd.
**Caption (IG) / description (Shorts):** line 1 = sawaal; phir 2–3 main sources (naam + saal); 3–5 niche hashtags.
**YouTube title:** curiosity + specific (number / naam / saal). Thumbnail text ≠ title.
**YouTube description:** script ke saare 🔗 links, line number ke saath (`--links` command se nikalo).
**On-screen:** har factual line par uska highlighted screenshot 2–3 sec (yahi aapka "receipts" brand hai).

## 7. Engagement loop

- Har video ke end mein **ek specific sawaal** (haan / na + reason).
- Pehle 60 min mein comments ke jawab do (sabse useful 10 ko pin / heart).
- Koi source maange toh description ka link do; screenshot already video mein hai.
- Galti pakdi jaaye toh agli reel ki shuruaat mein 10 sec ka **correction** (alag post nahi). Isse trust badhta hai.

## 9. Risk checklist (growth ko barbaad karne wali cheezein)

- ❌ Health / medical misinformation (community guidelines strike + trust loss)
- ❌ Communal / caste generalisation (strike, legal risk, brand-unsafe)
- ❌ Defamation: kisi insaan / company par bina court / official record ke ilzaam. Accused ko
  "aaropi" bolo, "doshi" nahi, jab tak court faisla na de.
- ❌ Copyrighted clips lambe (> few sec) bina commentary ke
- ❌ Undisclosed sponsorship (ASCI)
- ❌ Burnout: 3 long + 4 reels / week akele bahut hai. Editor ya edit-template ke bina Thu skip karo.
- ❌ Bina screenshot wali line: agar proof nahi, toh line nahi.
