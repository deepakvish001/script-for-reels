## B1 · It Was Never About The Product (planned obsolescence)

- **Titles:** (1) It Was Never About The Product (2) Aapka Phone Kharab Nahi Hota, Kharab *Kiya* Jaata Hai? (3) 1,000 Ghante Ka Sauda
- **Thumbnail:** "KHARAB YA KIYA GAYA?" + purana bulb + phone
- **Core sawaal:** kya companies jaan-boojh kar cheezein jaldi kharab karti hain, ya yeh conspiracy theory hai?
- **Cold open:** December 1924, Geneva. Duniya ki badi bulb companies ek meeting mein tay karti hain ki
  bulb 1,000 ghante se zyada nahi chalega (Phoebus cartel).
- **Foundation misaal:** "Razor aur blade": razor sasta, blade mehnga. Kamaai pehli khareed se nahi,
  baar-baar ki khareed se.
- **Story chain:**
  1. Phoebus cartel: zyada chalne wale bulb par jurmana (Krajewski, *IEEE Spectrum* 2014)
  2. iPhone slowdown: Apple ne purane phones ki battery ke hisaab se speed kam ki; US settlement (2020)
     aur France ka jurmana (2020)
  3. Printer aur ink cartridge; "Right to Repair" ki ladai (EU rules; India ka Right to Repair portal, 2022)
- **Twist:** har obsolescence conspiracy nahi. Kuch engineering trade-off hain (bulb: lambi life = kam roshni,
  zyada bijli). Farq tab hai jab **jurmana** ya **chhupaana** shaamil ho.
- **Doosri side:** "bathtub curve" (har machine ki failure ka natural pattern); naye features ke liye
  purana support band karna zaroori bhi hota hai.
- **Aapke liye:** battery health check, repairability score dekhna, warranty aur Right to Repair portal.
- **Seed sources:** Krajewski 2014 (IEEE Spectrum); Apple settlement court docs (N.D. Cal.); French
  DGCCRF release (Feb 2020); India Right to Repair portal (DoCA, 2022). **Overlap note:** bulb reel retired, yahin use karo.

## B2 · "Digital Arrest": Ek Video Call Jo Lakhon Le Gayi

- **Titles:** (1) Digital Arrest Ka Poora Khel (2) Police Video Call Par Arrest Nahi Karti. Phir Log Kyun Phanste Hain? (3) Darr Ka Business
- **Thumbnail:** "VIDEO CALL PE ARREST?" + phone screen par nakli "police"
- **Core sawaal:** padhe-likhe log bhi "digital arrest" mein kyun phans jaate hain?
- **Cold open:** ek asli, reported case (date + shehar + rakam) jisme peedit ne khud media se baat ki ho.
  Peedit ka naam tabhi jab woh khud public hua ho.
- **Foundation misaal:** "Exam hall ka darr": jab authority (uniform, court, CBI) samne ho, dimaag sochna
  band karke maanna shuru karta hai (authority bias).
- **Story chain:**
  1. Scam ka script: parcel / drugs / Aadhaar misuse → nakli police → "case hai" → "kisi ko mat batana" → "account verify karne ke liye paisa bhejo"
  2. Psychology: authority, isolation ("call mat kaato"), urgency, aur sharm (log baad mein bhi nahi batate)
  3. System: I4C / MHA ke numbers, mule accounts, aur sarkar ki advisories (PM ka "Ruko, Socho, Action Lo" message, Mann Ki Baat, Oct 2024)
- **Twist:** sabse bada hathiyaar technology nahi, **akelapan** hai. Jo call par akela rakha gaya, wahi phansa.
- **Doosri side:** bank aur telecom ki zimmedari kitni? Kuch log kehte hain "user ki galti". Dono taraf ka data.
- **Aapke liye:** 3 niyam (koi agency video call par arrest nahi karti; call kaato, 1930 par report karo,
  cybercrime.gov.in; ghar walon ko yeh video bhejo).
- **Seed sources:** I4C / MHA press releases (PIB), RBI advisories, 1930 helpline, Mann Ki Baat transcript (Oct 2024).

## B3 · Diamond "Rare" Kaise Bana?

- **Titles:** (1) Diamond Rare Nahi Hai (2) Ek Ad Line Ne Shaadi Ka Rivaaj Badal Diya (3) It Was Never About The Stone
- **Thumbnail:** "RARE YA MARKETING?" + ring
- **Core sawaal:** diamond mehnga kyun hai: kami ki wajah se, ya kami dikhane ki wajah se?
- **Cold open:** 1947, New York. Ek copywriter (Frances Gerety) ek line likhti hai: "A Diamond Is Forever."
- **Foundation misaal:** "Cinema hall ka popcorn": jab supply ek haath mein ho aur emotion saath ho, daam
  cost se nahi, feeling se tay hota hai.
- **Story chain:**
  1. De Beers aur supply control (Epstein, *The Atlantic*, Feb 1982)
  2. N.W. Ayer campaign: diamond = pyaar ka saboot; resale value ka sach
  3. Aaj: lab-grown diamond (chemically same), Surat ka cutting-polishing role, aur daam par asar
- **Twist:** jo cheez "forever" bechi gayi, uska resale sabse kamzor.
- **Doosri side:** bade, high-quality natural diamond sach mein kam milte hain; mining countries ki economy.
- **Aapke liye:** certificate (IGI / GIA), lab-grown vs natural ka farq, buy-back policy padho.
- **Seed sources:** Epstein 1982; De Beers / Anglo American annual reports; GJEPC export data; IGI / GIA guides.
  **Overlap note:** diamond reel retired, yahin use karo.

## B4 · Apps Aapko Kaise Phansate Hain

- **Titles:** (1) Aap Scroll Nahi Karte, Aapse Scroll Karwaya Jaata Hai (2) Infinite Scroll Banane Wale Ko Pachhtava Kyun Hai? (3) Dimaag Ka Jaal
- **Thumbnail:** "AAPSE KARWAYA JAATA HAI" + phone + slot machine
- **Core sawaal:** kya phone ki aadat hamari kamzori hai, ya design ka nateeja?
- **Cold open:** infinite scroll ke designer Aza Raskin ka public pachhtava (interviews, ~2019).
- **Foundation misaal:** slot machine: kabhi-kabhi inaam (variable reward) sabse zyada baandhta hai.
- **Story chain:**
  1. Variable reward: pull-to-refresh = slot machine (Schüll, *Addiction by Design*, 2012)
  2. Notifications, streaks, autoplay: kaunsa feature kya karta hai
  3. Research: social media chhodne ke experiments kya batate hain (Allcott et al.; 2024 PNAS studies)
- **Twist:** "free" app mein product aap nahi, **aapka time** hai.
- **Doosri side:** research mixed hai; har screen time bura nahi; kuch studies ka asar chhota.
- **Aapke liye:** notifications off, grayscale, app timer, "phone doosre kamre mein" experiment (7 din).
- **Seed sources:** Schüll 2012; Allcott, Gentzkow et al. (PNAS 2024); WSJ "Facebook Files" (2021); India DPDP Act 2023.

## B5 · Angrez Kitna Le Gaye? $45 Trillion Number Ka Sach

- **Titles:** (1) $45 Trillion: Angrez Kitna Le Gaye? (2) Woh Number Jo Har Debate Mein Aata Hai (3) Drain Ka Hisaab
- **Thumbnail:** "$45 TRILLION?" + purana map
- **Core sawaal:** yeh number kahan se aaya, aur kitna pakka hai?
- **Cold open:** 1901, Dadabhai Naoroji ki kitaab "Poverty and Un-British Rule in India" aur "drain" ka idea.
- **Foundation misaal:** "Ghar ka kiraya": aap kamao, aur hisaab doosre ke ghar jaye. Drain = kamaai ka bahar jaana.
- **Story chain:**
  1. Naoroji ka drain theory (1901)
  2. Utsa Patnaik ka estimate (~$45 trillion, 1765–1938) aur uska method
  3. Bengal famine 1943: research (Mishra et al. 2019, *GRL*) ke mutabik sookha main wajah nahi tha; Amartya Sen ka kaam
- **Twist:** number par ladne se zyada zaroori hai **method samajhna**.
- **Doosri side:** economic historians ki critique (compounding / interest-rate assumptions); railways aur institutions wali debate.
- **Aapke liye:** kisi bhi viral number par 3 sawaal: kisne nikala, kaise, kis assumption par.
- **Seed sources:** Naoroji 1901; Patnaik (Columbia UP essay, 2017/18); Mishra et al. 2019 (GRL); Sen, *Poverty and Famines* (1981). **Overlap note:** famine reel retired.
