# YouTube Long-Form DNA (16 asli videos se) + "Better" version

Source analysis: [`../research/05-youtube-long-form-transcripts.md`](../research/05-youtube-long-form-transcripts.md)
General framework: [`03-long-form-framework.md`](03-long-form-framework.md) · Template: [`../templates/long-video-script-template.md`](../templates/long-video-script-template.md)

> **Ek line mein:** *Date wali kahani → "simple nahi hai" → concept zero se ghar ki misaal ke saath
> → sponsor bridge → 2–4 kahaniyon ki chain → dots connect → naam/callback reveal → sawaal.*
> **Aapka edge:** har claim par receipt, ek "doosri side" section, aur zero health / communal misinformation.

---

## 1. Timeline (15–20 min, ~2,200–2,800 words)

| Time | Block | Kya karna hai | Line templates |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:40 | **Cold open** | Ek exact date / jagah / insaan wali kahani **ya** viral cheez **ya** research stat | "___ (date) ki baat hai. ___ mein ek ___ ne..." / "Kuch time pehle ek news viral hui thi ki ___" / "___ University ne ___ logon ko paise diye ki ___" |
| 0:40–1:30 | **Stakes + promise** | Common belief bolo aur todo; poori video ka promise | "Log sochte hain ___. Par aisa nahi hai." / "Last tak sunna, aapka ___ dekhne ka nazariya badal jaayega." |
| 1:30–3:30 | **Foundation** | Ek core concept zero se, ek ghar ki misaal ke saath | "Isse pehle ek cheez samajhna zaroori hai." / "Simple misaal deta hoon: ___" |
| 3:30–4:30 | **Sponsor (optional)** | Bridge → read → exit | "Jab bhi ___ hota hai, ___." → read → "Khair, wapas aate hain." |
| 4:30–13:00 | **Story chain** | 2–4 kahaniyan; har ek: kaun, kab, kya hua, "ab yeh kyun bata raha hoon?" | "Ab ek aur kahani suniye." / "Is bande ka naam yaad rakhna." / "Yeh kaun tha, yeh main aage bataunga." |
| 13:00–15:00 | **Doosri side** ⭐ (aapka edge) | Strongest counter-argument, imaandari se | "Ab fair rehte hain. ___ waale kehte hain ki ___, aur yeh point solid hai kyunki ___." |
| 15:00–17:00 | **Dots connect + payoff** | Pattern, callback, naam reveal | "Pattern dekho: ___, ___, ___." / "Woh insaan jiska naam yaad rakhne ko kaha tha? ___." |
| 17:00–18:30 | **Aapke liye** | 2–3 practical cheezein jo viewer kar sakta hai | "Toh aap kya kar sakte ho? Teen cheezein." |
| 18:30–19:00 | **Close** | Ek punch line + specific comment sawaal | "Ab aap batao: ___? Comment mein reason ke saath." |

## 2. Cold-open menu (16 videos se, aur behtar)

| Type | Unka example | Aapka better version |
|---|---|---|
| Dated story | "16 Sep 1973, Castro ka plane..." | Date + **source on screen** (archive photo / newspaper) |
| Celebrity news | "Virat ne 100 crore ka flat liya?" | Agar news fake hai toh pehle hi bolo "yeh fake thi", phir asli kahani |
| Research stat | "Stanford ne 35,000 logon ko paise diye" | Paper ka naam + saal screen par, 1 line mein method |
| Myth-bust | "Flight 513 wapas aayi? Fake hai." | Myth → kisne failaaya → asli science |
| Personal moment | "Main scroll kar raha tha / Rishikesh mein mila" | Sachchi, chhoti, specific. Kabhi banawati mat banao |
| Pop-culture sawaal | "Bigg Boss kyun dekhte ho?" | Trending show / IPL / film se universal psychology sawaal |

## 3. Foundation analogies bank (unki + nayi)

| Concept | Misaal |
|---|---|
| Pressure buildup / anger | Cooker ki seeti: seeti baad mein bajti hai, pressure pehle se banta hai |
| Inflation / Cantillon | 2 seb, 2 log, ₹10 each → sabko ₹20 do toh seb ₹20 ka |
| Observer effect (carefully!) | PUBG map tab load hota hai jab tum pahunchte ho (sirf misaal, physics claim nahi) |
| Emotions "real" nahi | Sapne mein dar lagta hai, heartbeat badhti hai, jabki kuch hua hi nahi |
| Capitalism ka self-eating loop | Snake game: badhta hai, phir apni poonchh kaat leta hai |
| Network effects | WhatsApp group: sab wahin hain, isliye sab wahin rehte hain |
| Economies of scale | Ek roti ke liye tandoor jalao vs 500 roti ke liye |
| Variable reward | Lottery / slot machine / "kabhi-kabhi reply" |
| Model collapse | Photocopy ki photocopy ki photocopy |

## 4. Sponsor integration (unka formula, aapka imaandar version)

1. **Placement:** 3:30–5:00 (hook ke baad, story chain se pehle). Kabhi cold open mein nahi.
2. **Bridge:** topic → universal line → sponsor ki category. Bridge forced ho toh sponsor ko end mein daalo.
3. **Read (45–60 sec):** problem → product → 2–3 specific features → offer → "link description mein".
4. **Disclosure:** "Yeh segment paid partnership hai." Platform ka paid-promotion toggle on rakho (ASCI guidelines).
5. **Claims:** "career 10x" jaise vaade mat karo. Wahi bolo jo aapne khud test kiya.
6. **Exit:** "Khair, wapas topic pe."
7. **Fit rule:** sirf woh brands jo aapke audience ki asli problem solve karte hain (upskilling, finance
   literacy, tools). Gambling / betting / unregulated finance (crypto pump, "guaranteed returns") **kabhi nahi**.

## 5. Delivery

- Talking head, natural bolchaal. Filler theek hai, par **har 60–90 sec par pattern break**
  (B-roll, map, headline, chhota sa joke, ek sawaal).
- "Main" se kahani: "maine padha", "mujhe laga", "main shock tha". Effort dikhao, ego nahi.
- Thoda humour aur sarcasm, **system par, logon par nahi** ([`10-humor-taunt-meme-bank.md`](10-humor-taunt-meme-bank.md)).
- Har naam, number ya date par screen par kuch aana chahiye.

## 6. Packaging (title + thumbnail)

**Unke title patterns:** "Dark Reality of ___", "The Dark Psychology of ___", "I Spent 7 Days Decoding ___",
"I tracked how ___", "Why Do They Want ___ Out?", "The End of ___", "It Was Never About ___".

**Aapke formulas:**
- "Ek aadmi ne ___ kaise kar diya" / "The Man Who ___"
- "___ ka asli bill kaun bharta hai?"
- "It Was Never About ___"
- "Maine ___ par 3 hafte research ki. Yeh mila."
- "___: Jo aapko school mein nahi bataya"

**Thumbnail:** chehra (ek strong emotion) + ek object / photo + max 3–4 bade shabd + ek red
arrow / circle. Title aur thumbnail ek doosre ko repeat na karein, balki complete karein.

## 7. Better-than-them checklist (har long-form)

- [ ] Fact sheet: har number ke 2 sources, links description mein
- [ ] "Doosri side" section (1–2 min)
- [ ] Allegation = "aarop hai", speculation = "mera andaza hai"
- [ ] Koi medical / safety advice nahi jo official guidance ke khilaaf ho
- [ ] Kisi community / religion / caste par blanket statement nahi
- [ ] Sponsor disclosure
- [ ] End mein "receipts" card (sources ka screenshot)
