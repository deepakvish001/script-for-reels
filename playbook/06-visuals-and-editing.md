# Visuals and Editing Guide

Rishabh ki videos ka visual grammar (scene analysis se):

## 1. Camera setup

| Format | Setup |
|---|---|
| Solo explainer | Medium close-up, eye level, lapel mic, bright neutral room (white wall + ek textured panel), simple dark ya printed shirt |
| Rant / reply short | Boom mic frame mein dikhe ("studio" feel), face close, zyada hand gestures |
| Podcast | Do chair, beech mein sofa ya lamp, warm side light, peeche curtain, 3 angles (wide + 2 singles) |

**Budget setup:** phone (1080p/4K) + ₹1–2k lapel mic + window light ya ek softbox + saaf deewar.
Chehre aur awaaz ki quality B-roll se zyada important hai.

## 2. B-roll types (sabse zyada se kam use)

1. **News headline screenshots.** CNBC, Zee News, etc. Yeh "yeh main nahi, news bol rahi hai"
   wali authority deta hai. Headline highlight karo, baaki blur.
2. **Real photos of people or events.** Split screen: left mein tum, right mein photo.
3. **Logos pop-in.** Jab list bol rahe ho (YouTube, Facebook, Instagram...), har naam par
   ek logo.
4. **Annotated photos.** Group photo par numbers 1–13, circles, arrows.
5. **Network / relationship diagram.** Beech mein ek entity, chaaron taraf connected logos.
6. **AI-generated concept images.** Abstract ideas ke liye ("raja aur crown", "digital brain",
   "suit wala aadmi + city skyline overlay").
7. **Stock footage.** Military parade, crowd, city. Mood ke liye.
8. **Icons.** Red question mark, warning sign, ✅/❌.

## 3. Pacing rules

- **Shorts:** har 2–4 sec mein visual change.
- **Long:** har 4–8 sec mein change. Har 60–90 sec mein pattern break (map, graph, full-screen
  quote, tone shift).
- Har **naam, number ya company** par screen par kuch aana chahiye.
- Jab tum **opinion** bol rahe ho, tab B-roll hata ke **face par aao**. Face = conviction.

## 4. Text and captions

- Shorts: bade, word-by-word captions. Har line ka key word (number, naam) alag rang mein.
- Long: lower-third mein sirf names, sources aur key numbers.
- **Source tag:** chhota, corner mein, 2–3 sec: `Source: NPCI, Oct 2026`.

## 5. Sound

- Long: halka background score, chapter change par music change.
- Shorts: trending ya tense instrumental, voice par duck (-18 to -24 dB under voice).
- "Reveal" moment par 0.5 sec silence ya bass hit.

## 6. Script → edit handoff format

Script do column mein likho taaki editor ko guess na karna pade:

| Voice (jo bologe) | Visual (editor ke liye) |
|---|---|
| "PayPal Mafia. Pata nahi aapne yeh naam suna hai ya nahi..." | Face MCU. Text: "PAYPAL MAFIA?" |
| "...yeh photo jo aapke saamne aa rahi hai..." | Full-screen group photo, slow zoom |
| "YouTube, Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn..." | Logos pop har naam par |

## 7. Free / cheap tools

- Editing: CapCut, DaVinci Resolve (free)
- Captions: CapCut auto-captions (Hinglish ko manually fix karo)
- Images: Canva, AI image generators (concept art ke liye; asli logon ki fake photos mat banao)
- Headlines: asli article ka screenshot lo, date dikhe
