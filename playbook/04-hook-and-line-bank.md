# Hook and Line Bank (Hinglish)

`___` ki jagah apna topic bharo. Har pattern ke saath likha hai ki woh kab kaam karta hai.
Rishabh ki videos se seedha nikle patterns par ⭐ laga hai.

---

## 1. Opening hooks (0–3 sec)

### Mystery label ⭐
- "___. Pata nahi aapne yeh naam suna hai ya nahi, par sunna chahiye."
- "Ek group hai jise duniya ___ ke naam se jaanti hai."
- "Is photo mein ___ log hain. Inhone milke aapki zindagi badal di, aur aapko pata bhi nahi."

### Relevance shock
- "Aapke phone ki ___ apps ek hi ___ ki hain."
- "Aap jo ___ roz khaate / use karte ho, uska asli maalik koi aur hai."
- "Aapki salary ka ___% aapse puche bina ___ mein chala jaata hai."

### Hidden layer ⭐
- "Jo naam aapne suna hai woh khiladi nahi hai. Asli khiladi woh hai jiska naam aapne kabhi nahi suna."
- "___ ko sab jaante hain. Par ___ ko koi nahi jaanta, aur wahi zyada powerful hai."

### Expectation flip ⭐
- "Aapko lagega yeh banda ___ hoga. Nahi. Yeh sirf ek ___ hai."
- "Sabko lagta hai ___ ki wajah se hua. Asli wajah bilkul ulti thi."

### Question (provocative) ⭐
- "___ kab bolta hai? Jab uspe ___ ka pressure hota hai?"
- "Agar ___ itna hi achha hai, toh ___ kyun nahi kar paaye?"
- "Ek sawaal: ___ ke paise kahan se aate hain?"

### Number / stat
- "₹0 fee. Phir bhi yeh company ___ crore kama rahi hai. Kaise?"
- "Sirf ___ saal mein ___ se ___ tak."

### Personal stake
- "Main ___ mein bada hua hoon. Isliye jab koi ___ bolta hai, mujhe chubhta hai."
- "Maine ___ hafte sirf is ek sawaal par lagaaye."

## 2. Transition / bridge lines ⭐

- "Isko samajhne ke liye humein pehle ___ mein jaana padega."
- "Ab yahan se kahani interesting hoti hai."
- "Par ruko. Asli khel toh iske baad shuru hua."
- "Ek example deta hoon, aap khud samajh jaoge."
- "Software kis cheez ka? Batata hoon." (khud sawaal, khud jawaab)
- "Aap soch rahe hoge ___. Main bhi yahi soch raha tha, jab tak maine ___ nahi padha."
- "Yeh point yaad rakhna, video ke end mein kaam aayega."

## 3. Research authority lines ⭐

- "Jab maine is par padhna shuru kiya, har jagah ek hi kahani mili."
- "Par yeh kahani yeh explain nahi karti ki ___."
- "Maine dono taraf ke sources padhe: ___ bhi aur ___ bhi."
- "Yeh main nahi bol raha, yeh ___ ki report bol rahi hai." (screen par dikhao)
- "Record mein yeh likha hai. Par ground par jo hota hai woh ___."

## 4. Fairness / steelman lines (aapka edge)

- "Ab doosri side ka sabse strong argument sunte hain, imaandari se."
- "Yahan ___ waale sahi hain, main maanta hoon. Par ___."
- "Main galat ho sakta hoon. Agar ho, toh comment mein source ke saath batao."

## 5. Thesis / punch lines

- "Duniya woh nahi chalate jo ___ jaante hain. Duniya woh chalate hain jo ___ samajhte hain." ⭐
- "Free cheez ka bill hamesha kahin aur chukaaya jaata hai."
- "Jo dikhta hai woh product hai. Jo chhupa hai woh business hai."
- "Power ka pehla rule: log aapko jaanein, usse pehle aap unhe jaan lo."

## 6. Value promise lines ⭐

- "Is kahani mein kuch aise facts hain jo aap apni daily life mein lagaoge toh bahut kaam aayenge."
- "Video ke end tak aap ___ ko kabhi pehle jaisa nahi dekhoge."

## 7. Closing / CTA lines

- "Ab aap batao: ___? Comment mein likho, main padhta hoon."
- "Agar is video ne aapki soch thodi bhi badli, toh ise us ek dost ko bhejo jo ___."
- "Part 2 mein: ___. Woh isse bhi zyada shocking hai."
- (Short ke liye) Last line = punch line. Uske baad kuch nahi. Cut.

**Middle (batch 2)**
