# Long-Form Framework (YouTube explainer, 12–30 min)

Rishabh ka long-form "**documentary-lite**" style hai: ek insaan camera se baat karta hai, aur
B-roll, headlines aur graphics se kahani aage badhti hai. Unke apne shabdon mein unka ideal
woh creator hai jo "**koi loophole nahi chhodta**", chahe mahine mein ek hi video aaye.

---

## 1. Master structure

```
[0:00–0:30]  COLD OPEN    : mystery label + visual proof + relevance bridge
[0:30–2:00]  HOOK STACK   : hidden layer, shocking example, expectation flip
[2:00–3:00]  JOURNEY      : "maine padha... sab yeh bol rahe the... par asli wajah kuch aur thi"
[3:00–3:30]  THESIS       : ek bold universal line + personal value promise
[3:30–4:00]  ROADMAP      : 3–4 sawaal + "isko samajhne ke liye pehle..."
[4:00–...]   CHAPTERS     : 3–5 chapters, chronological ya cause→effect
[last 3 min] PAYOFF       : thesis wapas, saare dots jodo, "aapke liye iska matlab"
[last 30s]   CLOSE        : ek yaad rehne wali line + sawaal for comments
```

Yeh structure **PayPal Mafia** video ke pehle 2 minute se seedha nikla hai. Unhone 2:05 tak
beats 1 se 10 complete kar diye the. Detail: [`../research/02-video-breakdowns.md`](../research/02-video-breakdowns.md)

---

## 2. Har section detail mein

### A. Cold open (0:00–0:30)
- **Line 1:** topic ka label + "aapne suna hai ya nahi, par sunna chahiye" type FOMO.
- **Visual 1:** asli photo, headline ya document, pehle 5 sec mein.
- **Relevance:** viewer ki life ki 3–5 cheezein ek-ek karke dikhao (logos ya icons pop karo).

### B. Hook stack (0:30–2:00)
Teen escalation, ek ke baad ek:
1. **Hidden layer:** "Jo naam aapne nahi suna, woh zyada powerful hai."
2. **Shocking example:** famous event se link ("Osama bin Laden isi software se mila").
3. **Expectation flip:** "Aapko lagega yeh ___ hoga. Par nahi, yeh ___ hai."

### C. Research journey (2:00–3:00)
- "Jab maine is par padhna shuru kiya, har jagah ek hi kahani mili: ___."
- "Par yeh kahani ___ explain nahi karti."
- "Jab mujhe asli wajah mili, tab maine yeh video banana shuru kiya."

Yahan tum **apna research effort dikhate ho**: kitni reports padhi, kis-kis paksh ke source
dekhe. Yahi trust banata hai.

### D. Thesis + value promise (3:00–3:30)
- **Thesis:** ek universal line. "Duniya ke sabse powerful log ___ nahi, ___ samajhte hain."
- **Promise:** "Is kahani mein kuch aise facts hain jo aap apni life mein lagaoge toh ___."

### E. Roadmap (3:30–4:00)
- 3–4 sawaal jo video answer karega. Yeh **open loops** hain, isliye inhe close karna zaroori hai.
- Bridge line: "Isko samajhne ke liye humein pehle ___ mein jaana padega."

### F. Chapters (body)
Har chapter ka mini-structure:

| Step | Kaam |
|---|---|
| Chapter question | "Toh PayPal ke andar us waqt kya hua?" |
| Story | Log, jagah, saal. Insaanon ke through batao, sirf data se nahi |
| Evidence | 1–3 facts, har ek ka source on screen |
| "Iska matlab kya hai?" | Interpretation, yahan tumhari opinion aati hai |
| Mini-reveal | Ek naya surprising fact |
| Open next loop | "Par asli khel toh iske baad shuru hua..." |

**Retention rule:** har 60–90 sec mein ek **pattern break** chahiye: naya visual style,
sawaal, joke, map, graph ya "ruko, yeh important hai."

**Steelman chapter (zaroor rakho):** ek chapter ya section jahan doosre paksh ka *sabse
strong* argument imaandari se rakho, phir batao tum kyun agree ya disagree karte ho. Isse
tum Rishabh se aage ke "thoughtful" creator bante ho, aur critics ka sabse bada hathiyaar
("biased") kamzor ho jaata hai.

### G. Payoff (last 3 min)
- Cold open ki photo ya sawaal **wapas laao**. Loop band karo.
- Thesis dobara bolo, ab evidence ke saath.
- "Aapke liye iska matlab": 2–3 concrete actions ya mindset shifts.

### H. Close (last 30s)
- Ek line jo short ban sake.
- Ek specific comment sawaal: "Aapke hisaab se ___? Comment mein batao." ("Like share
  subscribe" se zyada kaam karta hai.)
- Agle video ka teaser (series feel).

---

## 3. Script length guide

Hinglish mein aaram se bolne par **~130–150 shabd per minute** hote hain.

| Video length | Script words | Chapters |
|---|---|---|
| 8–10 min | 1,100–1,400 | 3 |
| 12–15 min | 1,700–2,200 | 4 |
| 20–25 min | 2,800–3,600 | 5 |

---

## 4. Podcast / interview format

Dusre podcasts par unke appearance se yeh dikha:
- **Teaser cold open:** podcast ki sabse spicy 4–6 lines ka montage, pehle 60 sec mein
  ("Mujhe rightist hone mein koi problem nahi..." / "Modi ke baare mein kuch galat bol do,
  famous ho jaoge").
- Phir branded intro animation.
- Host research method, rivals, aur personal stand par sawaal karta hai.

Agar tum podcast karo: **teaser montage + chapter timestamps + har spicy answer ka alag short.**

---

## 5. Production checklist (long)

- [ ] Title: curiosity + specific ("PayPal Mafia: 13 log jo aapka phone chalate hain")
- [ ] Thumbnail: face (emotion) + 1 strong visual + max 3–4 shabd
- [ ] Script mein har claim ke aage `[SRC: ...]` likha ho
- [ ] B-roll list har paragraph ke saath (see [`06-visuals-and-editing.md`](06-visuals-and-editing.md))
- [ ] Chapters / timestamps description mein
- [ ] Description mein **sources ki list**. Yahi tumhara trust badge hai
- [ ] Video se 3–5 shorts plan kiye ([`02-short-form-framework.md`](02-short-form-framework.md))
