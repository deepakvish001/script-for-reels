# Topic Idea Bank

Rishabh ke content pillars ke hisaab se ideas. Har idea ke saath ek **mystery label**,
**core question** aur **thesis direction** di hai. Research ke baad thesis badal sakti hai,
aur badalni bhi chahiye agar evidence kuch aur bole.

## Pillar 1: Hidden systems / power ("kaun chala raha hai?")

| Label (hook) | Core question | Thesis direction |
|---|---|---|
| "UPI ka free trap" | UPI free hai toh kamaata kaun hai? | Free = bill kahin aur (example: [`../examples/`](../examples/)) |
| "Index fund ke maalik" | Kya 3 fund companies duniya ki har badi company ki maalik hain? | Ownership ≠ control, par voting power asli hai |
| "Credit score ka khel" | CIBIL score kaun banata hai, aur aapko kaise judge karta hai? | Aapki ek galti 7 saal follow karti hai |
| "Cricket ka paisa" | BCCI duniya ka sabse ameer board kaise bana? | TV rights = asli game |
| "Quick commerce ka sach" | 10 minute delivery kaise possible hai, aur kiski keemat par? | Speed ka kharcha dark stores aur riders uthaate hain |
| "Algorithm ka feed" | Aapka feed aapko kyun gussa dilaata hai? | Engagement = anger; human brain hi product hai |

## Pillar 5: Rebuttal / response (kabhi-kabhi, carefully)

- Kisi viral claim ka **fact-check**: "Yeh viral video ___ kehti hai. Maine check kiya."
- Kisi bade creator ki galti: **unka clip + source + correction**, personal attack nahi.
- "Dono side ka sach": ek mudde par left aur right dono ke best arguments, phir tumhara view.

## Idea scoring (shoot karne se pehle)

Har idea ko 1–5 par score karo:

| Factor | Sawaal |
|---|---|
| Curiosity | Kya title sunke "yeh kya hai?" lagta hai? |
| Relevance | Kya viewer ki jeb, phone, job ya family se juda hai? |
| Contradiction | Kya koi popular belief toot raha hai? |
| Evidence | Kya 8+ achhe sources mil sakte hain? |
| Takeaway | Kya viewer kuch "kar" sakta hai iske baad? |

**20+ score** wale idea pehle banao.
