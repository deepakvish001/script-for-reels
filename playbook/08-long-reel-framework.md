# Long-Reel Framework (Instagram, 2–3 min): Rishabh ka asli reel format

5 asli reels ke analysis se bana ([`../research/03-instagram-reels-analysis.md`](../research/03-instagram-reels-analysis.md)).
Unki Instagram reels **60 sec ki nahi hain. Har reel 2.5–3 minute ki mini-explainer hai**,
jo ek dost ki tarah phone par baat karte hue banti hai.

---

## 1. Format specs

| Cheez | Unka tareeka | Kyun kaam karta hai |
|---|---|---|
| Camera | Phone ka **front camera**, haath mein, thoda neeche se | "Dost video call kar raha hai" wala feel. Production ki deewar nahi |
| Location | **Gaadi** (zyada tar), ya **bed par lete hue** | Casual, "abhi-abhi socha toh bata raha hoon" vibe, aur har jagah shoot ho sakta hai |
| Outfit | Rugby / polo shirt (green, navy, black) | Recognisable look, ek tarah ki uniform |
| Lighting | Din mein khidki ki roshni, raat mein dim car light | Natural. Raat ki reel zyada "secret baat" jaisi lagti hai |
| Captions | **Burned-in captions nahi** | Face aur B-roll par focus. (Aapke liye: captions lagao, isse reach badhti hai) |
| Length | 2:29 – 3:00 | Itna time ki ek poora argument aa jaaye, par YouTube jitna commitment na lage |
| B-roll share | ~35–45% screen time | Har 6–10 sec mein face se hatke proof ya image |

## 2. Visual system (har reel mein repeat)

1. **News headline screenshots + yellow highlighter** (scroll.in, Hindustan Times, Moneycontrol,
   NYT, CBS, Times of India). Key phrase par **progressive highlight** hota hai, jo bolne ke
   saath badhta hai.
2. **Black / dark text cards**, AI-answer style: numbered heading ("1. Project: ..."), bullet facts,
   aur kabhi source chip.
3. **AI-generated illustrations**: protest crowd, CBI raid, refinery + bottle collage,
   bachche + glowing brain, township renders. Jahan asli photo na ho wahan concept dikhane ke liye.
4. **Label overlays**: "FAKE!", "SOVEREIGNTY". Ek shabd, serif font, black box.
5. **Original clips**: Army officer ka video, celebrity interview, brand ads.
6. **Stock chart / data screenshot**: Google Finance chart, revenue card.
7. **B/W filter on face**: emotional ya serious point par 1–2 sec.
8. **Self-callback split-screen**: "On 31st October" ke saath apni purani video ka frame aur us
   din ka chart (prediction sahi nikli, yeh dikhane ke liye).

## 3. Master structure: "Mini-explainer" (R1, R3, R5 se)

```
[0:00–0:15]  HOOK         : news peg / number shock / celebrity, aur turant "simple nahi hai bhai"
[0:15–0:45]  CONTEXT      : players aur stakes (do desh, do company, purana vs naya)
[0:45–1:05]  PRINCIPLE    : ek universal law ("jo pehle infra banayega, uska upper hand")
[1:05–1:40]  MECHANISM    : asal mein kaise hua (step-by-step, "yeh X business nahi, Y business hai")
[1:40–2:00]  REVEAL       : "Guess karo..." → ek surprising fact (FIR, cost, ownership)
[2:00–2:30]  ZOOM OUT     : bada picture (geopolitics, desh, future), dots jodna
[2:30–3:00]  CLOSE        : request / prediction / values line, aur English mein ek punch line
```

### Beat-wise lines (unke patterns se)

| Beat | Pattern line |
|---|---|
| Hook | "Abhi jo ___ viral hua tha na, woh fake tha." / "___ sirf ₹___ mein liya tha. Kya hota hai bhai ₹___?" |
| Anti-simple | "Aapko lagta hai simple cheez hai? Simple nahi hai bhai." |
| Context | "Do ___ hain. Ek ___, doosra ___." |
| Principle | "Yahan sabse important pata kya hota hai? Jo ___, uska clear-cut upper hand." |
| Mechanism | "Yeh ___ ka business actually ___ ka business nahi hai. Yeh hai ___ business." |
| Reveal | "Aur yeh hua kaise? ... Guess karo, ___." |
| Asymmetry | "Unhe kisi se poochna nahi hai. Hamein clearance, court, public, sab dekhna padta hai." |
| Dots | "Theek usi time ___ hua. Yeh saare coincidences nahi the." |
| Objection | "Ab kai logon ko mirchi lagti hai ki yeh ___ ki tareef kyun kar raha hai..." |
| Close | "Meri bas itni si request hai..." / "Time ke saath dekhte rehna." / "It's a new kind of ___." |

## 4. Variant A: Inspiration bridge (R4)

```
Celebrity hook ("___ ke baare mein pata hai? Unki real story kam logon ko pata hai")
→ emotional origin (ek specific dard)
→ mission + design principles
→ result (numbers)
→ PIVOT: "Aapko pata hai India mein isse kahin badi..."
→ Indian version + scale comparison (numbers side by side)
→ features
→ values close (dignity, apnapan)
```

**Fair-play rule:** compare like with like (chalta project vs chalta project). Agar ek sirf
announce hua hai, toh saaf bolo.

## 5. Variant B: Brand integration (R2)

```
Curiosity hook jo brand chhupaye ("yeh celebrities kya leke ghoom rahe hain?")
→ "Achha yeh chhodo..." → naam wala concept (bliss point) + authority card
→ villain category (normal ketchup), brand ka naam nahi
→ "revolution aa raha hai"
→ hook ka jawaab: founder story (parent + bachcha)
→ spec comparison (54% vs 28–30%)
→ mission line
→ CTA (link in bio, family ko batao)
```

**Rules:** "Paid partnership" / #ad label lagao (ASCI). Product ki kamzori bhi imaandari se bolo
(jaise "gud bhi sugar hi hai, kam khao"). Celebrity endorsement tabhi imply karo jab sach ho.

---

## 6. Delivery style

- **"Bhai"** se seedha address: "simple nahi hai bhai", "yahi toh saari kahani hai na bhai".
- **"Na" fillers** jo rhythm banate hain: "abhi yahan pe na...", "woh jo hote hain na...".
- **Haath ke gestures** bahut. Gaadi mein ek haath free rehta hai, aur woh beat ke saath chalta hai.
- **Aankhein side mein** (sochte hue), phir camera mein (punch line par).
- **Last line English mein** ("It's a new kind of warfare that goes on." /
  "We should all come back to the real food."). Hinglish reel ka English close quote jaisa lagta hai.
- **Scripted nahi lagta, par structured hai.** Beats fix hote hain, shabd natural.

## 7. Aapke liye upgrade checklist (unse behtar karne ke liye)

- [ ] Har number jo bolo, wahi screen card par ho (R1 mein "2030" bola, card par "2033" tha)
- [ ] Allegation ko allegation bolo ("CBI ka aarop hai...", "FIR mein claim hai...")
- [ ] Correlation ko coordination mat bolo. "Sawaal yeh uthta hai ki..." zyada imaandaar hai
- [ ] Comparison fair ho (announced vs operational)
- [ ] Sponsored reel par disclosure
- [ ] Captions lagao. Unke reels mein nahi hain, aur yeh aapka easy edge hai
- [ ] Ek "doosri side" line, 10 sec ki bhi chalegi
