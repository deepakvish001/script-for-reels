# Short-Form Framework (Reels / Shorts, 30–90 sec)

> **Note:** Rishabh ki asli Instagram reels **2.5–3 min** ki hoti hain. Woh format
> [`08-long-reel-framework.md`](08-long-reel-framework.md) mein hai. Yeh file 30–90 sec ke
> Shorts / Reels ke liye hai (YouTube Shorts, ya long video se kaate hue clips).

Short mein **ek hi idea** hota hai. Usse ek **sawaal → twist → proof → punch** ki tarah
bolna hai. Rishabh ke shorts do tarah ke dikhe:

1. **Explainer short:** long video ka condensed version (mystery, phir reveal).
2. **Argument / rebuttal short:** ek strong opinion, rhetorical sawaalon ki chain
   ("Media kab bolti hai? ... Aisa banda comment karne ka deserve kaise karta hai?").

Dono ke liye niche ka framework kaam karega.

---

## 1. The 6-beat reel structure (60 sec ka example timing)

| Beat | Time | Kaam | Example line pattern |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1. Hook** | 0–3s | Scroll rokna. Ek sawaal, ek shocking claim ya ek mystery label | "Aapke phone ki 5 apps ek hi group ke 13 logon ne banaayi hain." |
| **2. Stakes** | 3–8s | "Mujhe kyun parwaah karni chahiye?" | "Aur yahi log decide karte hain ki aap din ke 4 ghante kahan bitaoge." |
| **3. Common belief** | 8–15s | Jo sab maante hain woh bolo | "Sab kehte hain yeh log genius engineers the." |
| **4. Twist** | 15–30s | Belief todo | "Par inke sabse powerful member ne coding kabhi ki hi nahi. Woh ek philosopher hai." |
| **5. Proof and explain** | 30–50s | 1–2 facts, screen par source, ek example | "Unki company Palantir ka software... [headline on screen]" |
| **6. Punch and loop** | 50–60s | Ek yaad rehne wali line, aur comment ya part-2 trigger | "Duniya woh nahi chalate jo code jaante hain. Woh chalate hain jo **aapka dimaag** jaante hain. Part 2 mein batata hoon kaise." |

**30 sec version:** Beat 3 hata do, beat 5 mein sirf 1 fact.
**90 sec version:** Beat 5 mein 2 examples, aur end se pehle ek "aap kya kar sakte ho" takeaway.

---

## 2. Hook rules (pehle 3 second sab kuch hain)

1. **Pehle hi frame par bolna shuru karo.** "Hi guys" ya intro nahi.
2. **Screen par 4–7 shabd ka text hook** jo spoken hook se match ho, par usse copy na ho.
3. **Number ya naam do.** "13 log", "₹0 fee", "PayPal Mafia". Specific cheez vague se zyada
   rokti hai.
4. **"Aap" ko andar laao.** "*Aapke* phone mein", "*aapki* salary".
5. **Pehla visual proof wala ho:** photo, headline, document, ya creator ka expressive face.

Poora hook bank: [`04-hook-and-line-bank.md`](04-hook-and-line-bank.md)

## 3. Argument / rebuttal short ka structure

Yeh Rishabh ka "reply" format hai: conflict, phir rhetorical sawaal, phir moral stand.

| Beat | Kaam | Pattern |
|---|---|---|
| Context | Kisne kya bola (clip ya screenshot) | "Inhone kaha ki ___. Aur mujhe reply kiya meri chhoti si clip kaat ke." |
| Concede | Thoda maan lo, isse fair lagoge | "Theek hai, data ke hisaab se yeh sahi hai..." |
| Counter | Asli point | "Par jo insaan ___ mein kabhi raha hi nahi, woh ___ ka dard kaise samjhega?" |
| Evidence | Ek ground example ya data | "Main ___ mein bada hua hoon. Wahan ___ hota hai." / "Report ___ kehti hai..." |
| Sawaal chain | 2–3 rhetorical sawaal | "Sirf akhbaar padh ke? Sirf research paper padh ke? Kaise?" |
| Stand | Clear position, ek line | "Main yeh bolta hoon aur bolta rahunga: ___." |

**Guardrails:** idea ko attack karo, insaan ki personal life ko nahi. Unka asli clip dikhao,
out-of-context mat kaato. Jo tum dusron par ilzaam lagaate ho, wahi galti khud mat karo.
Community par generalisation nahi.

---

## 4. Long video se short kaise kaatein (repurposing)

Har long video se **3–5 shorts** nikaalo:

1. **Hook short:** long video ka intro (mystery → twist), end mein "poori kahani channel par."
2. **Shocking-fact short:** sabse "kya?!" wala ek fact, uska source aur example.
3. **Thesis short:** video ka bold universal law, aur ek example.
4. **Takeaway short:** "Aap apni life mein yeh 1 cheez karo." Yeh sabse zyada save hota hai.
5. **Opinion short:** tumhara sabse strong opinion. Yeh sabse zyada comment laata hai.

Har short ko **re-script** karo. Seedha clip kaatne se aksar context ki zaroorat padti hai
jo short mein nahi hota. Naya 2-sec hook record karo aur clip ke aage lagao.

---

## 5. On-screen and edit checklist (short)

- [ ] 0s par face ya proof, aur text hook
- [ ] Har 2–4 sec mein visual change (zoom-in, B-roll, headline, logo)
- [ ] Word-by-word captions (Hinglish, Roman script; key shabd highlight)
- [ ] Har fact ke saath source ka chhota tag (e.g. "Source: RBI 2025")
- [ ] Background music halka, bolne par duck
- [ ] Last line par cut. Outro mat lagao. Loop ban jaaye toh best
- [ ] Caption mein: 1 sawaal (comment ke liye) + 3–5 niche hashtags
