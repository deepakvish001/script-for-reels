# Humor, Taunt, Meme aur Common Sense Bank

Rishabh ki videos mein humour halka par lagataar hai ("mahanubhav", "bhai saahab", "gidh", "dimag
se halka", "gormint" wali vibe). Humour **retention** badhata hai: viewer has-ta hai, toh rukta hai.
Yeh bank aapko apna andaaz banane ke liye hai.

## 1. Golden rules

1. **Punch up, never down.** System, sarkaari daftar, corporations, ads, apni khud ki galtiyan:
   haan. Gareeb, kamzor, kisi caste / dharm / gender / body / disability par: **nahi**.
2. **Joke se fact nahi badalta.** Joke ke baad turant asli point par aao.
3. **Har 60–90 sec mein ek halki line** (long-form). Reel mein 1–2 kaafi hain.
4. **Self-roast safe hai.** "Maine bhi yahi galti ki thi" se trust badhta hai.
5. **Naam leke mazaak sirf public figures / brands ke public actions par**, personal life par nahi.
6. **Meme reference ho, meme copy nahi.** Dialogue ka 3–5 shabd ka reference theek hai. Poora clip
   lagana copyright claim la sakta hai.

## 2. Ready lines (Hinglish)

**System / sarkaar / corporate par**
- "Itna ghuma diya ki Google Maps bhi bole: 'recalculating'."
- "Terms and conditions itne lambe hain ki padhte-padhte warranty khatam ho jaaye."
- "Free hai, bas aapka data, aapka time aur aapki neend lagegi."
- "Company ne kaha 'customer is king'. King ko IVR mein 9 dabane ko bola."
- "Committee bani, phir committee ki report par ek aur committee bani."
- "Yeh business model nahi, jaadu hai: product free, profit full."

**Science / myth par**
- "WhatsApp University ke Vice Chancellor ne yeh fact bheja tha."
- "Physics ko itna stretch kiya ki Einstein bhi bole: 'bhai, main nahi tha.'"
- "Coincidence itne zyada ho gaye ki ab coincidence bhi shak karne laga."

**Self-roast**
- "Maine bhi 3 saal yahi socha. IQ points ka hisaab mat poochna."
- "Research karte-karte itne tab khule ki laptop ne mujhse research shuru kar di."
- "Haan haan, main bhi reel scroll kar raha tha. Isliye toh pata hai."

**Audience se masti (respectful)**
- "Jo log 'skip sponsor' dabane wale the, ruk jao, yeh wala kaam ka hai." (sirf tab jab sach ho)
- "Comment mein 'haldi' likh do agar aapne bhi ghar mein sabse chamakdaar haldi kharidi thi."

## 3. Meme / pop-culture hooks (2026 Indian internet)

| Format | Kab use karein | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Film dialogue reference | Twist / reveal par | "Picture abhi baaki hai mere dost." |
| Cricket analogy | Strategy / competition samjhaane ko | "Yeh test match nahi, T20 hai: jo pehle scale karega, wahi jeetega." |
| Exam / coaching jokes | Youth topics | "Paper leak hua, aur students se kaha 'dobara padh lo, character build hoga.'" |
| Shaadi / rishtedaar | Social pressure topics | "Sharma ji ka beta bhi isi scheme mein phansa tha." |
| Corporate jargon parody | Business topics | "Synergy, leverage, paradigm shift... matlab daam badhenge." |

*Trending memes har hafte badalte hain. "Trend scan" process [`../growth/00-90-day-growth-plan.md`](../growth/00-90-day-growth-plan.md) mein hai.*

## 4. Taunt vs insult: farq

| ✅ Taunt (theek) | ❌ Insult (nahi) |
|---|---|
| "Kampani ne bulb ki umar 1000 ghante fix ki. Shayad bulb ki bhi retirement policy thi." | Kisi group ko "bewakoof" bolna |
| "Pollution par meeting AC room mein hui, kyunki bahar saans nahi aa rahi thi." | Kisi neta ki shakal / body par joke |
| "Algorithm ko aapse zyada aapki aadaton ka pata hai." | Kisi rival creator ko gaali |

## 5. Common-sense check (har script se pehle)

- Kya ek 15 saal ka bachcha aur ek 60 saal ke uncle dono yeh line samjhenge?
- Kya yeh joke kal screenshot hokar context ke bina viral ho toh mujhe sharminda karega?
- Kya joke ke baad fact sahi hai?
